# Knowledge Processing Playbook — Document Use-Case Patterns

## What this is, and how to read it

This playbook organizes "complete knowledge processing" on a knowledge-management platform by the **use case** a document serves rather than by its format. It is built on top of the descriptive `knowledge-management-wiki` (the Wave 5 `knowledge-processing` domain): every claim here is drawn from ingested literature and linked inline to its KB source page (relative links into the repo).

It is deliberately **neutral**. It reports where the literature *converges* and where it *diverges*; it does **not** prescribe "for your platform, do X." Comparative results ("outperforms", "best", "worse", "SOTA") are always the *cited study's* finding under its own conditions, not a recommendation. Treat every convergence as "what multiple sources independently lean toward," and every divergence as a genuine open choice you own.

Two scoping notes carried from the Wave 5 agreement:
- **Document nature is an attribute, not an axis.** Structure, normativity, volatility, sensitivity, provenance-need, and lifecycle *condition* each use-case pattern; they appear as annotations, not rows.
- **Audience is parked.** Persona (→ entitlement/visibility) and expertise novice→expert (→ adaptation/framing) are a documented future overlay; DITA conditional processing/profiling ([dita](../sources/standard/dita.md)) and the ROME novice/intermediate/expert personas (Wave 3) are the anchors when it is added.

The five use-case profiles: **issue-resolution**, **obligation-lookup**, **eligibility-determination**, **extraction-verification**, **compliance-checking**.

## The lifecycle spine

Every profile fills in the same nine-stage spine. The playbook's value is which stages carry the weight for each use case:

`acquire/classify → parse/segment → extract → model & link → provenance/metadata → govern → serve/retrieve → evaluate → maintain`

## Profiles, walked through the lifecycle

### Issue-resolution (help & service)
Defining query: "how do I do / fix X." Document nature: prose, informational, low sensitivity, moderate volatility. The literature here is methodological rather than model-centric. KCS frames knowledge as a **by-product of solving, created in the workflow, demand-driven** (from actual questions, not anticipated needs), then reused and improved in place, with a separate Evolve loop for content health ([kcs](../sources/provider-doc/kcs.md)) [note: KCS double-loop specifics are `[gen]` in the KB — verify against the KCS Practices Guide]. DITA supplies the complementary information architecture: type content by its *nature* — concept / task / reference — and profile a single source into audience-specific renditions ([dita](../sources/standard/dita.md)) [`[gen]`, spec unreachable at ingest]. Convergence: structure the content and drive creation from demand; the two sources are complementary layers (methodology + information typing), not competitors. Weight lands on serve/retrieve (self-service deflection) and maintain (content health).

### Obligation-lookup (policies, contracts, regulation)
Defining query: "what am I bound to / what does this clause say." Document nature: clausal, binding, entity-rich, high provenance need. The dominant convergence is **precision with provenance**: LegalBench-RAG argues for retrieving *minimal, highly relevant snippets* over large chunks or document IDs, labeling every answer with the exact file and character span so an LLM can cite and a human can verify ([legalbench-rag](../sources/article/legalbench-rag.md)). Extraction is treated as obligation identification — EURO-5K as sentence-level supervised extraction of EU reporting obligations ([euro-5k-obligation-extraction](../sources/article/euro-5k-obligation-extraction.md)); De Jure as decomposition into structured multi-field rules ([de-jure-regulatory-rules](../sources/article/de-jure-regulatory-rules.md)). A notable *divergence/caution* to surface, not resolve: LegalBench-RAG reports a general-purpose reranker performing **worse** than none on its legal benchmark, which it attributes to domain mismatch ([legalbench-rag](../sources/article/legalbench-rag.md)) — a caution about porting general RAG components into a specialized domain, stated as their result. Weight: parse/segment, extract, precise retrieval, provenance.

### Eligibility-determination (benefits, entitlements)
Defining query: "does person P qualify for B." Document nature: rule-bearing, high volatility (rates/thresholds), conditional logic. This profile is where the weight shifts *off* retrieval and *onto rule evaluation over facts*. The convergent thread is **rules-as-code**: OpenFisca models legislation as parameters (legal values tracked over time) and formulas, computing eligibility for one person or millions ([openfisca](../sources/provider-doc/openfisca.md)); De Jure and the public-sector neuro-symbolic work push the same text→executable-rule direction, the latter emphasizing that a determination's *explanation must reflect the legal rule that authorizes it* ([de-jure-regulatory-rules](../sources/article/de-jure-regulatory-rules.md))([neurosymbolic-public-sector-eligibility](../sources/article/neurosymbolic-public-sector-eligibility.md)). The sharpest *caution* in the whole wave lives here: "Confidently Wrong" documents **exception-chain collapse** — frontier LLMs failing on nested conditional rules of the form "A required UNLESS B" — which the paper offers as an argument for deterministic/symbolic execution ([exception-chain-collapse](../sources/article/exception-chain-collapse.md)). The KB records this as the paper's finding; the playbook surfaces it as a real risk you must weigh, not a verdict. Weight: extract→model (rules), serve (evaluation), maintain (versioning volatile params).

### Extraction-verification (identity, onboarding, forms)
Defining query: "pull the fields / verify authenticity." Document nature: structured/scanned forms, heavy PII. Two convergences. First, **localization before reasoning**: the KYC multistage pipeline reports page-level retrieval as the dominant factor, reserving expensive VLM extraction for the few relevant pages, with mandatory human-in-the-loop review framed as an existing regulatory requirement rather than added cost ([kyc-multistage-extraction](../sources/article/kyc-multistage-extraction.md)); element/structure-aware chunking similarly outperforms fixed-size on structured financial documents in its study ([financial-report-chunking](../sources/article/financial-report-chunking.md)). Second, **authenticity is its own task**: EdgeDoc treats ID-document forgery as detection *and* localization ([edgedoc-id-forgery](../sources/article/edgedoc-id-forgery.md)). Weight: parse (OCR/preprocess), retrieve (page-level), extract (fields), verify (forgery + human review), govern (PII).

### Compliance-checking (minimum wage, labor law, regulatory)
Defining query: "is jurisdiction S conformant with norm N as of date D." Document nature: obligation-bearing, jurisdiction-scoped, high volatility, audit-critical. This is the top of the ladder (below): obligations are extracted into machine-readable norms ([de-jure-regulatory-rules](../sources/article/de-jure-regulatory-rules.md)) and assessed for conformance. ComplianceNLP frames the serve stage as **regulatory gap detection** — classifying obligation↔policy pairs as compliant / partial gap / full gap over a regulatory knowledge graph with KG-augmented RAG ([compliancenlp-gap-detection](../sources/article/compliancenlp-gap-detection.md)). Weight: extract→model (norms + KG), serve (gap assessment), maintain (regulatory-event tracking), evaluate (gap F1).

## Cross-cutting convergences (recurring across profiles)

1. **Structure beats flat.** Chunking/parsing that respects document structure (element type, layout, headings, tables) is reported to beat fixed-size splitting wherever it is compared ([financial-report-chunking](../sources/article/financial-report-chunking.md)) (and, from Wave 4, ([gcp-parse-chunk](../sources/provider-doc/gcp-parse-chunk.md))([document-parsing-rag-omdena](../sources/blog/document-parsing-rag-omdena.md))). See `[structure-aware-chunking](../concepts/structure-aware-chunking.md)` / `[semantic-chunking](../concepts/semantic-chunking.md)`.
2. **Precision + provenance + evidence-grounding.** Retrieve minimal, cite to source span, ground generation in traceable evidence — LegalBench-RAG's snippet-precision ([legalbench-rag](../sources/article/legalbench-rag.md)) and MedGraphRAG's `[RAG-entity, source, definition]` triple linking to controlled vocabularies ([medical-graph-rag](../sources/article/medical-graph-rag.md)) are the same instinct in two domains.
3. **The obligation → eligibility → compliance ladder.** Retrieve text → evaluate rules over facts → assess conformance, each adding machinery on the last; **rules-as-code / machine-readable norms** recur at the top two rungs, with symbolic execution offered for determinism ([openfisca](../sources/provider-doc/openfisca.md))([de-jure-regulatory-rules](../sources/article/de-jure-regulatory-rules.md)) and LLM rule-evaluation fragility flagged as the counterweight ([exception-chain-collapse](../sources/article/exception-chain-collapse.md)).
4. **Human-in-the-loop for high-stakes.** Mandatory review in KYC ([kyc-multistage-extraction](../sources/article/kyc-multistage-extraction.md)) and clinician evaluation in medical RAG ([medical-graph-rag](../sources/article/medical-graph-rag.md)) — see `[human-in-the-loop-verification](../concepts/human-in-the-loop-verification.md)`.
5. **Domain adaptation is task-dependent.** Domain-specific RAG (linking to domain sources/controlled vocabularies) outperforms generic in its own domain in the cited studies ([medical-graph-rag](../sources/article/medical-graph-rag.md)), while EURO-5K asks *when* domain pretraining actually matters and reports the gain is not always significant ([euro-5k-obligation-extraction](../sources/article/euro-5k-obligation-extraction.md)). The literature does not settle this — it is a genuine per-task judgment.

## The extract stage up close — how the LLM/agent actually does it

The matrix's *extract* stage is where most of the AI work happens; the KB's `ai-technique` concepts (in `knowledge-processing`) record how, and where it breaks:

- **Schema-guided / structured output** ([schema-guided-extraction](../concepts/schema-guided-extraction.md), [structured-output-generation](../concepts/structured-output-generation.md)). Give the model a target schema and constrain the output. The key reported finding to set expectations by: models hit **near-perfect schema *compliance* but far lower value *accuracy*** — best exact leaf-value match ~83% (text) / ~67% (image) / ~24% (audio) ([structured-output-benchmark](../sources/article/structured-output-benchmark.md)). Valid-JSON ≠ correct-values. Large schemas need pruning ([schemarag](../sources/article/schemarag.md): +8.8% F1, −47% latency, −48% cost); and invalid-schema output is itself a failure mode for weaker models ([idp-accelerator](../sources/article/idp-accelerator.md)).
- **Complex tables** ([complex-table-understanding](../concepts/complex-table-understanding.md), [table-serialization](../concepts/table-serialization.md)). The recurring bottleneck is *serialization*: flattening nested/multi-header/merged-cell tables into token sequences loses the structure the answer depends on ([st-raptor](../sources/article/st-raptor.md), [astra-table-qa](../sources/article/astra-table-qa.md)). Layout-aware tree representations + human-correctable structure are the reported mitigations ([st-raptor](../sources/article/st-raptor.md): +11.2% over the best baseline).
- **Agentic extraction** ([agentic-extraction](../concepts/agentic-extraction.md), [agentic-reflection-control](../concepts/agentic-reflection-control.md)). Multi-step agents that plan, use tools, and self-correct — but with a sobering caveat: on one controlled study, reflection+memory raised coverage only modestly (158→168 records, +10) while log volume rose 7× (7,084→50,189 lines), and LLM reflection added just 3 records over rule reflection ([agentic-controllability-ie](../sources/article/agentic-controllability-ie.md)). Agents buy *controllability/observability* more clearly than accuracy; and generic agent designs fail on real enterprise KGs unless domain structure is encoded ([scair](../sources/article/scair.md)).
- **Self-verification & provenance** ([extraction-self-verification](../concepts/extraction-self-verification.md), [provenance-constrained-extraction](../concepts/provenance-constrained-extraction.md)). Forward/backward answer checks, confidence-gated human review, and evidence ledgers that constrain outputs to tool-produced provenance ([st-raptor](../sources/article/st-raptor.md), [idp-accelerator](../sources/article/idp-accelerator.md), [ledgermind](../sources/article/ledgermind.md)) — the reliability layer that makes the *extract* stage trustworthy enough to reduce human review.

## Where the literature diverges / cautions (leave standing)

- **General-purpose components in specialized domains** can hurt: the legal-domain reranker result ([legalbench-rag](../sources/article/legalbench-rag.md)).
- **LLMs on nested conditional rules** are fragile (exception-chain collapse) ([exception-chain-collapse](../sources/article/exception-chain-collapse.md)); the eligibility literature leans symbolic/deterministic for auditability, but this is an argument, not a consensus.
- **Provenance of two sources is weaker**: KCS double-loop detail and the entire DITA page are `[gen]` (primary text unreachable at ingest) — verify before relying.
- **Metrics are not comparable across profiles**: deflection rate, Precision/Recall@k, field-level accuracy, gap F1, and forgery AUC measure different things; there is no single "knowledge-processing score."
- **Valid structure ≠ correct content**: near-perfect schema compliance coexists with much lower value accuracy ([structured-output-generation](../concepts/structured-output-generation.md)); and adding agentic reflection/memory can multiply process complexity without a proportional accuracy gain ([agentic-reflection-control](../concepts/agentic-reflection-control.md)) — so "the JSON validated" and "we added an agent" are not evidence the extraction is right.

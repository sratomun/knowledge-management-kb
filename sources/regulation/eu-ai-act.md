# EU Artificial Intelligence Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689)

> _Authored from general knowledge, not from the primary text. Verify against the primary source (the Official Journal text) before relying on specifics, article numbers, or dates._

## Scope & purpose
The EU AI Act is the European Union's horizontal, risk-based regulation of artificial intelligence systems placed on the EU market or whose output is used in the EU. It classifies AI uses into risk tiers and attaches obligations proportionate to risk, with the stated aim of promoting trustworthy AI while protecting health, safety, and fundamental rights. It is captured here **lightweight** as governance context for a knowledge-management platform, not as a deep clause-by-clause ingest.

## Key points
- The Act takes a **risk-based** approach, sorting AI uses into: unacceptable-risk (prohibited), high-risk (permitted subject to requirements), limited-risk (transparency obligations), and minimal-risk (largely unregulated) ⟨Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, risk framework⟩ [gen]
- Certain practices are **prohibited**, including specified social scoring by public authorities, certain manipulative/exploitative techniques, and (with exceptions) untargeted scraping of facial images and some real-time remote biometric identification in public spaces ⟨Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Art. 5⟩ [gen]
- **High-risk** AI systems carry obligations such as risk management, data governance, technical documentation, record-keeping/logging, transparency, human oversight, and accuracy/robustness/cybersecurity ⟨Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Title III⟩ [gen]
- Providers of **general-purpose AI (GPAI) models** have specific transparency and documentation obligations, with additional obligations for models deemed to pose systemic risk ⟨Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, GPAI provisions⟩ [gen]
- The Act entered into force in 2024 with a **staggered application timeline**: prohibitions apply first, GPAI obligations later, and most high-risk obligations phase in over roughly two to three years ⟨Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, entry-into-application schedule⟩ [gen]
- Enforcement combines national market-surveillance authorities with a European **AI Office**; penalties are tiered, with the highest fines for prohibited-practice violations ⟨Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, penalties⟩ [gen]

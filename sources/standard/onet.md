# O*NET (Occupational Information Network)

> _Authored from general knowledge, not from the primary text. Verify against the primary O*NET documentation before relying on specifics or counts._

## Scope & purpose
O*NET is the primary U.S. source of occupational information: a database describing occupations in terms of the knowledge, skills, abilities, tasks, and work activities they require, sponsored by the U.S. Department of Labor. Captured here **lightweight** as an occupation/skills taxonomy relevant to HR knowledge modeling.

## Key points
- Organizes work using the **O*NET-SOC** occupational taxonomy, derived from and cross-walked to the federal Standard Occupational Classification (SOC) ⟨O*NET documentation, occupational taxonomy⟩ [gen]
- Describes each occupation through the **O*NET Content Model**, spanning worker characteristics, worker requirements, experience requirements, occupational requirements, workforce characteristics, and occupation-specific information ⟨O*NET Content Model⟩ [gen]
- Provides structured descriptors for **knowledge, skills, abilities (KSAs), tasks, tools/technology, and work activities** per occupation ⟨O*NET descriptors⟩ [gen]
- Data are published openly (O*NET database and O*NET OnLine) and updated on a rolling basis, supporting career exploration, job analysis, and workforce systems ⟨O*NET database releases⟩ [gen]

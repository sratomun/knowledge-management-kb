# ISO/IEC 27001 — Information security management systems

> _Authored from general knowledge, not from the primary text. Verify against the primary source (the purchased ISO/IEC 27001:2022 standard) before relying on specifics or control numbers._

## Scope & purpose
ISO/IEC 27001 specifies requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an **information security management system (ISMS)**. It is the certifiable standard in the ISO/IEC 27000 family. Captured here **lightweight** as governance context for securing a knowledge-management platform and its catalog.

## Key points
- The standard specifies requirements for an **ISMS** within the context of the organization, following the common ISO management-system structure (context, leadership, planning, support, operation, performance evaluation, improvement) ⟨ISO/IEC 27001:2022, Clauses 4–10⟩ [gen]
- It requires a **risk-based** approach: information security risk assessment and risk treatment tied to the organization's objectives ⟨ISO/IEC 27001:2022, Clause 6⟩ [gen]
- Annex A provides a **catalog of controls**; the 2022 edition reorganizes them into four themes — organizational, people, physical, and technological — comprising 93 controls ⟨ISO/IEC 27001:2022, Annex A⟩ [gen]
- Organizations produce a **Statement of Applicability** justifying which Annex A controls are included or excluded ⟨ISO/IEC 27001:2022, Clause 6.1.3⟩ [gen]
- The detailed control guidance for Annex A lives in the companion **ISO/IEC 27002** code of practice ⟨ISO/IEC 27002:2022⟩ [gen]

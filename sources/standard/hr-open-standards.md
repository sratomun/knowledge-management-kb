# HR Open Standards

> _Authored from general knowledge, not from the primary text. Verify against the primary HR Open Standards documentation before relying on specifics._

## Scope & purpose
HR Open Standards is a consortium-maintained set of open data-interchange standards for human-resources processes (recruiting, assessment, staffing, payroll, benefits), historically known as HR-XML. Captured here **lightweight** as an HR data-interoperability standard.

## Key points
- Publishes free, vendor-neutral **data-exchange schemas** for HR business processes to enable interoperability between HR systems and service providers ⟨HR Open Standards documentation⟩ [gen]
- Is the successor to the earlier **HR-XML** library, modernized under the HR Open Standards Consortium ⟨HR Open Standards, history⟩ [gen]
- Provides standardized message/schema definitions (e.g., JSON/XML) for objects such as candidates, assessments, and staffing orders ⟨HR Open Standards schemas⟩ [gen]
- Aims to reduce point-to-point integration cost across the HR technology ecosystem ⟨HR Open Standards, purpose⟩ [gen]

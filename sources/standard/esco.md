# ESCO (European Skills, Competences, Qualifications and Occupations)

> _Authored from general knowledge, not from the primary text. Verify against the primary ESCO documentation before relying on specifics or counts._

## Scope & purpose
ESCO is the European multilingual classification of skills, competences, qualifications, and occupations, maintained by the European Commission to support labour-market and education interoperability across the EU. Captured here **lightweight** as an occupation/skills taxonomy relevant to HR knowledge modeling.

## Key points
- Structures the labour market into three interlinked **pillars**: occupations, skills/competences, and qualifications ⟨ESCO documentation, three pillars⟩ [gen]
- Is **multilingual**, publishing concepts and preferred/alternative labels across many EU languages to enable cross-border matching ⟨ESCO documentation, multilinguality⟩ [gen]
- Maps its occupations to the international **ISCO-08** occupational classification for interoperability ⟨ESCO documentation, ISCO mapping⟩ [gen]
- Is published as **linked open data** with a SPARQL endpoint and downloadable datasets, using stable URIs for concepts ⟨ESCO linked data⟩ [gen]

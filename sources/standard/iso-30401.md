# ISO 30401:2018 - Knowledge management systems - Requirements

## Scope & purpose

> Note: the full normative text of ISO 30401:2018 is paywalled and was not consulted. Key points below come from (1) secondary sources — the official ISO scope (iso.org/standard/68683) and RealKM (Shekar, 2022-02-02) — and (2) general knowledge of the standard; they are not quoted from the primary clauses.

ISO 30401:2018 sets requirements and offers guidance for establishing, implementing, maintaining, reviewing, and continually improving an effective management system for knowledge management within organizations. It applies to any organization regardless of type or size. The intent is to help organizations develop a knowledge management system (KMS) that promotes value creation from organizational knowledge, providing a common framework and vocabulary so that KM programs can be started and matured regardless of the specific KM solution implemented.

## Structure

ISO 30401:2018 follows the ISO Harmonized Structure (High-Level Structure) common to all ISO management-system standards, so its requirements are organized into the same numbered clauses as ISO 9001, ISO 27001, and similar standards:

- Clauses 1-3: Scope, Normative references, and Terms and definitions (front matter; clauses 1-3 are not auditable requirements).
- Clause 4 — Context of the organization: understanding the organization and its context, needs and expectations of interested parties, scope of the KMS, and the KMS itself (including the knowledge development lifecycle and knowledge conveyance/enablers).
- Clause 5 — Leadership: leadership and commitment, knowledge management policy, and organizational roles, responsibilities and authorities.
- Clause 6 — Planning: actions to address risks and opportunities, and knowledge management objectives and planning to achieve them.
- Clause 7 — Support: resources, competence, awareness, communication, and documented information.
- Clause 8 — Operation: operational planning and control of the KMS.
- Clause 9 — Performance evaluation: monitoring, measurement, analysis and evaluation; internal audit; and management review.
- Clause 10 — Improvement: nonconformity and corrective action, and continual improvement.

The standard also includes informative annexes: Annex A on the knowledge spectrum (the range from data/information through explicit and tacit knowledge), Annex B on the relationship between knowledge management and adjacent disciplines, and Annex C on knowledge management culture. The underlying knowledge-conveyance model draws on the SECI (Socialization, Externalization, Combination, Internalization) model of knowledge creation.

Related maintenance and revision activity: Amendment 2022 (ISO 30401:2018/Amd 1:2022) and a further Amendment 2024 updated the standard, primarily to align its structure and text with the current ISO Harmonized Structure; a full revision is in progress as ISO/DIS 30401 (the draft international standard for a second edition).

## Key points

- ISO 30401:2018 specifies requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining, reviewing and improving an effective knowledge management system in organizations. ⟨iso.org/standard/68683⟩
- The standard applies to any organization regardless of its type, size, or the products and services it provides. ⟨iso.org/standard/68683⟩
- All requirements are generic and intended to be applicable to any organization, not to a specific industry or sector. ⟨iso.org/standard/68683⟩
- Every organization should develop its own KMS framework aligned with the ISO 30401 KMS standard requirements, rather than adopting a single prescribed solution. ⟨RealKM (Shekar, 2022)⟩
- The knowledge management system has multiple dimensions that need to be established, implemented, maintained, and continuously improved. ⟨RealKM (Shekar, 2022)⟩
- The standard's requirement clauses can be mapped to a KMS design framework to help organizations align their KM program with the standard. ⟨RealKM (Shekar, 2022)⟩
- ISO 30401 requirements broadly follow the typical KM execution lifecycle: initiation and sponsorship, planning requirements, design of practices/processes/IT, implementation (testing, training, documentation), go-live support, and measurement of usage and effectiveness. ⟨RealKM (Shekar, 2022)⟩
- Continuous improvement of the KM system evolves as user feedback is channeled back into systematic development. ⟨RealKM (Shekar, 2022)⟩
- The standard provides a framework for individuals and organizations to get started with KM programs and follow the KM lifecycle regardless of the KM solution implemented. ⟨RealKM (Shekar, 2022)⟩
- ISO 30401 is a suitable starting point when an organization wants to begin KM or needs to define KM to management and the board, providing guidance on how to develop KM systems. ⟨RealKM (Shekar, 2022)⟩
- The standard promotes value creation from organizational knowledge as the central aim of the knowledge management system. ⟨iso.org/standard/68683⟩
- ISO 30401 is a certifiable management-system standard, so organizations can be audited and certified against its requirements. ⟨iso.org/standard/68683⟩

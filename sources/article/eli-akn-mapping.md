# Mapping ELI and Akoma Ntoso for Legal-Ontology Interoperability

> _Authored from general knowledge, not from the primary text (ACM page was not retrievable). Verify against the primary source before relying on specifics._

## Scope & purpose

An academic article addressing interoperability between two legal-informatics standards: the **European Legislation Identifier (ELI)**, an RDF-based scheme for identifying and describing legislation by URI plus metadata, and **Akoma Ntoso (LegalDocML)**, an XML vocabulary for the structure and content of legal documents. The work concerns how the metadata/identification layer of ELI can be aligned or mapped with the document-model layer of Akoma Ntoso so that legal resources are consistently referenced and described across systems ⟨general knowledge⟩.

## Key points

- Motivates alignment between ELI (identifier + metadata ontology for legislation) and Akoma Ntoso (XML document model), which address complementary but distinct layers of the legal-document stack [gen] ⟨general knowledge⟩.
- Both standards draw on FRBR-style abstraction (work / expression / manifestation), providing a natural anchor for mapping identifiers and versions between the two vocabularies [gen] ⟨general knowledge⟩.
- Discusses mapping ELI metadata properties to Akoma Ntoso's metadata/identification constructs to enable round-tripping between RDF descriptions and XML markup [gen] ⟨general knowledge⟩.
- Frames the mapping as a contribution to legal-ontology interoperability and Linked Open Data reuse of legislative resources across jurisdictions [gen] ⟨general knowledge⟩.
- Positions the mapping as infrastructure for cross-system legal information retrieval and citation, reducing fragmentation between identifier-based and document-based tooling [gen] ⟨general knowledge⟩.

# Nonaka & Takeuchi — The Knowledge-Creating Company (SECI)

> _Authored from general knowledge, not from the primary text. Verify against the primary source (Nonaka & Takeuchi 1995) before relying on specifics._

## Scope & purpose
Ikujiro Nonaka and Hirotaka Takeuchi's account of organizational knowledge creation, which introduced the **SECI model** of how tacit and explicit knowledge convert through four modes. Captured here **lightweight** to ground the existing `seci-model` entity in its originating source (previously cited only via ISO 30401).

## Key points
- Distinguishes **tacit** knowledge (personal, context-specific, hard to formalize) from **explicit** knowledge (codified, transmittable in formal language) ⟨Nonaka & Takeuchi 1995, knowledge typology⟩ [gen]
- Proposes the **SECI** spiral of four conversion modes: **Socialization** (tacit→tacit), **Externalization** (tacit→explicit), **Combination** (explicit→explicit), and **Internalization** (explicit→tacit) ⟨Nonaka & Takeuchi 1995, SECI⟩ [gen]
- Frames knowledge creation as a continuous **spiral** amplifying knowledge across individual, group, organizational, and inter-organizational levels ⟨Nonaka & Takeuchi 1995, knowledge spiral⟩ [gen]
- Later work (Nonaka & Konno) adds **"ba"**, the shared context or place in which knowledge conversion occurs ⟨Nonaka & Konno, concept of ba⟩ [gen]

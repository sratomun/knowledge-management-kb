# Amazon Comprehend — Product Overview

## Scope & purpose
This page captures the Amazon Comprehend product overview at the technique level, recording
the natural-language text-analysis capabilities it embodies as a form of document-metadata
extraction. Honesty note: the product page's feature bullets are rendered client-side and
did not fully load, so only the tagline, benefits, and use cases below are primary text;
the capability list is marked [gen] (general knowledge, to be verified against the primary
page/docs) and its bullets are tagged accordingly. Consistent with the KB's technique-first
framing, no vendor product entity is created and the provider is referenced only in the
trailer. Provider-specific solution depth and cross-provider comparison are deferred to
Wave 5.

## Key points
- Comprehend is a natural language processing (NLP) service positioned to "derive and understand valuable insights from text within documents" ⟨Comprehend overview: tagline⟩.
- Listed benefits are valuable insights, simplify, differentiate your business, and protect and control access ⟨Comprehend overview: Benefits⟩.
- Listed use cases are mining business and call-center analytics, indexing and searching product reviews, legal-briefs management, and processing financial documents ⟨Comprehend overview: Use cases⟩.
- Vendor-cited customers include LexisNexis (insights from legal documents faster), Chisel AI (automating document-intensive processes), and TeraDact (safe environment for sharing information) ⟨Comprehend overview: Customers⟩.
- [gen] Comprehend's NLP capabilities typically include entity recognition, key phrase extraction, and sentiment and targeted-sentiment analysis — text-derived metadata extraction ⟨Comprehend overview: capability list [gen]⟩.
- [gen] Further capabilities typically include language detection, syntax analysis, and topic modeling over document collections ⟨Comprehend overview: capability list [gen]⟩.
- [gen] Capabilities also typically include PII detection and redaction, plus custom classification and custom entity recognition trained on user data ⟨Comprehend overview: capability list [gen]⟩.

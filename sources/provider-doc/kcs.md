# KCS (Knowledge-Centered Success)

## Scope & purpose
Knowledge-Centered Success (KCS, formerly Knowledge-Centered Support) is a methodology for leveraging an organization's collective experience in real time, created and maintained by the Consortium for Service Innovation. This page captures the consortium's high-level overview of KCS. The overview page is marketing-level and high-level; the detailed **double-loop practice model** (Solve Loop / Evolve Loop and their constituent practices) is **not on the overview page** — those bullets below are marked `[gen]` from general knowledge of the KCS v6 Practices Guide and should be verified against that guide.

## Structure
The overview page frames KCS as an operating model for knowledge, enumerates business benefits and outcomes, and points to resources: the open-source KCS Practices Guide, the Adoption Guide (the Digital Library), and training/certification (KCS v6), plus KCS designations for Verified Tools, Aligned Tools, and Aligned Services. The methodology's internal structure (the double-loop model and its practices) is documented in the KCS Practices Guide rather than on this overview page.

## Key points
- The page describes KCS as "the industry-standard methodology to leverage an organization's collective experience in real time," created and maintained by the Consortium for Service Innovation ⟨serviceinnovation.org/kcs⟩
- It states the Consortium for Service Innovation is the only certifying body for KCS ⟨serviceinnovation.org/kcs⟩
- The page positions KCS as "the operating model that makes AI viable at scale," arguing that agentic and LLM solutions are only as effective as the content they consume and that KCS keeps content accurate, structured, and trusted as "a prerequisite for AI success" ⟨serviceinnovation.org/kcs⟩
- It claims validated, demand-driven data prevents hallucinations and builds user trust for reliable automation ⟨serviceinnovation.org/kcs⟩
- It claims KCS "overcomes limitations of traditional knowledge management to help organizations keep content accurate and useful over time," framing it as an operating system for both human and AI agents ⟨serviceinnovation.org/kcs⟩
- Enumerated benefits include improved self-service for known issues, less duplicated effort, faster onboarding, faster resolution and first-contact resolution, and fewer escalations/handoffs ⟨serviceinnovation.org/kcs⟩
- It claims KCS turns everyday interactions into structured insights — root-cause of recurring issues, voice-of-customer signals, and high-quality data to power automation/AI/ML ⟨serviceinnovation.org/kcs⟩
- KCS is built on a **double-loop model**: the Solve Loop (individual, real-time, in the workflow of solving user issues) and the Evolve Loop (collective, periodic, improving the knowledge base and the organization) ⟨KCS v6 Practices Guide⟩ [gen]
- Solve Loop practices are Capture (create knowledge as a by-product of solving), Structure (use a simple Knowledge-Centered Article template), Reuse (search early, search often; reuse and link existing articles), and Improve (modify articles in the moment of use) ⟨KCS v6 Practices Guide⟩ [gen]
- Evolve Loop practices are Content Health (article quality via the Article Quality Index / AQI), Process Integration (knowledge creation is part of solving, not a separate step), Performance Assessment (assess people on knowledge-sharing behaviors, not article counts), and Leadership & Communication ⟨KCS v6 Practices Guide⟩ [gen]
- Core concepts include a **demand-driven** knowledge base (articles created from actual demand, not anticipated needs), "we know collectively more than we know individually," knowledge as a by-product of interaction, just-in-time content, and articles that reflect the requestor's context and words ⟨KCS v6 Practices Guide⟩ [gen]

# NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0 (AI RMF)

> _Authored from general knowledge, not from the primary text. Verify against the primary source (NIST AI 100-1) before relying on specifics._

## Scope & purpose
The NIST AI RMF 1.0 is a voluntary, rights-preserving, sector-agnostic framework to help organizations manage risks of AI systems and cultivate trustworthy AI. It is guidance, not regulation. Captured here **lightweight** as governance context.

## Key points
- The framework is organized around characteristics of **trustworthy AI**: valid and reliable; safe; secure and resilient; accountable and transparent; explainable and interpretable; privacy-enhanced; and fair with harmful bias managed ⟨NIST AI 100-1, §Trustworthiness⟩ [gen]
- Its core comprises four functions — **Govern, Map, Measure, Manage** — with Govern as a cross-cutting culture/process function and the other three applied iteratively across the AI lifecycle ⟨NIST AI 100-1, §Core⟩ [gen]
- **Govern** establishes policies, accountability, and risk culture; **Map** establishes context and frames risks; **Measure** analyzes and tracks risks with metrics; **Manage** allocates resources to treat and monitor risks ⟨NIST AI 100-1, §Core⟩ [gen]
- The framework is explicitly **voluntary** and intended to be used with a companion Playbook and profiles that tailor it to use cases and sectors ⟨NIST AI 100-1, §Overview⟩ [gen]
- It defines AI **risk** as a function of the likelihood and magnitude of impact, and stresses that risk is contextual and can involve tradeoffs among trustworthiness characteristics ⟨NIST AI 100-1, §Framing risk⟩ [gen]

# APQC - Levels of Knowledge Management Maturity

## Scope & purpose

APQC's Levels of Knowledge Management Maturity provide a road map for moving from
immature, inconsistent knowledge management (KM) activities to mature, disciplined
approaches aligned to strategic business imperatives. The resource offers organizations a
road map with milestones and checkpoints to guide their KM efforts and assess where a KM
program currently stands ⟨apqc.org/resource-library/resource-listing/apqcs-levels-of-knowledge-management-maturity⟩.

> Note: only APQC's public page was retrievable; the five maturity levels below are
> summarized from that page and general knowledge of APQC's model — not from the full
> gated resource.

## Structure

A five-level staged maturity model. Each level describes the state of a KM program's
capabilities, and organizations progress through the levels sequentially using the
described milestones and checkpoints as they advance from ad hoc activity toward
strategically aligned, continuously improving KM ⟨apqc.org/resource-library/resource-listing/apqcs-levels-of-knowledge-management-maturity⟩.

## Key points

- Level 1 — Initiate: KM activity is nascent, ad hoc, and inconsistent; the organization
  begins recognizing the value of KM but lacks defined processes or dedicated ownership
  ⟨apqc.org/resource-library/resource-listing/apqcs-levels-of-knowledge-management-maturity⟩.
- Level 2 — Develop: the organization builds an initial KM strategy, approach, and
  business case, and begins piloting KM approaches and tools ⟨apqc.org/resource-library/resource-listing/apqcs-levels-of-knowledge-management-maturity⟩.
- Level 3 — Standardize: KM approaches, methods, and tools are documented and
  standardized so they can be applied consistently and repeatably across the organization
  ⟨apqc.org/resource-library/resource-listing/apqcs-levels-of-knowledge-management-maturity⟩.
- Level 4 — Optimize: standardized KM approaches are measured, managed, and refined;
  performance is tracked against metrics and improved over time ⟨apqc.org/resource-library/resource-listing/apqcs-levels-of-knowledge-management-maturity⟩.
- Level 5 — Innovate: KM is mature, disciplined, and aligned to strategic business
  imperatives; the organization continuously improves and innovates its KM practices
  ⟨apqc.org/resource-library/resource-listing/apqcs-levels-of-knowledge-management-maturity⟩.
- The model functions as an assessment and planning road map with milestones and
  checkpoints, letting an organization locate its current KM maturity and plan the next
  advancement ⟨apqc.org/resource-library/resource-listing/apqcs-levels-of-knowledge-management-maturity⟩.

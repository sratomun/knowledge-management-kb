# DAMA-DMBOK2: Data Management Body of Knowledge (2nd ed.)

## Scope & purpose

> Note: DMBOK2 is a paid book and was not consulted. Key points below come from (1) a secondary source — the DAMA overview page (dama.org) — and (2) general knowledge of the DAMA-DMBOK framework and the DAMA Wheel; they are not quoted from the book's text.

The DAMA-DMBOK2 is the globally recognized reference framework for the discipline of data management, published by DAMA International. It defines a consensus-based, vendor-neutral view of generally accepted knowledge in data management, establishing a common vocabulary, scope, and boundaries for the profession. It is explicitly not a prescriptive standard, a regulation, a technology manual, an exhaustive knowledge repository, a certification program, or a one-size-fits-all approach; organizations adapt its guidance to their own environments and maturity levels. It serves as the foundational knowledge base for the CDMP certification and is designed to evolve over time (the 2.0 Revision launched in 2024 and a 3.0 evergreening initiative started in 2025).

## Structure

DAMA-DMBOK2 organizes the discipline around the **DAMA Wheel**: Data Governance sits at the hub as the central function that coordinates and provides oversight, surrounded by ten additional knowledge areas arranged around it. The eleven knowledge areas are: Data Governance (hub), Data Architecture, Data Modeling & Design, Data Storage & Operations, Data Security, Data Integration & Interoperability, Document & Content Management, Reference & Master Data (Reference Data Management and Master Data Management), Data Warehousing & Business Intelligence, Metadata Management, and Data Quality.

Each knowledge area is treated as a chapter and described using a consistent context-diagram template covering: **goals and principles**, **inputs**, **activities** (planning, control, development, operational), **deliverables**, **roles** (suppliers, participants, consumers), **tools and techniques**, and **metrics**. The framework is complemented by cross-cutting chapters on data-management ethics, big data & data science, data-management maturity assessment, organization & role expectations, and data-management strategy/organizational change.

## Key points

- The DAMA-DMBOK is a curated, structured subset of generally accepted data-management knowledge that defines the scope and boundaries of the profession rather than an exhaustive inventory ⟨dama.org⟩
- It reflects a consensus-based view designed to remain flexible and evolve with technological and methodological advancements ⟨dama.org⟩
- The 2nd edition adds expanded data ethics coverage, updated data governance content, new insights into data integration & interoperability, and enhanced focus on emerging technologies ⟨dama.org⟩
- It helps organizations structure, govern, and optimize data assets, aligning them with business strategy, regulatory compliance, and emerging technologies such as AI, machine learning, and cloud computing ⟨dama.org⟩
- Standardization: it establishes a common language and structured framework for data-management professionals worldwide ⟨dama.org⟩
- Governance & compliance: it provides guidance on data security, privacy, and regulatory adherence in a complex data landscape ⟨dama.org⟩
- Strategic alignment: it helps treat data as a valuable strategic asset integrated with business objectives ⟨dama.org⟩
- It provides the knowledge base for the CDMP certification and other educational programs but is not itself a certification or training program ⟨dama.org⟩
- It is vendor-neutral and does not prescribe specific tools, technologies, or methodologies; organizations must adapt it to their unique environments ⟨dama.org⟩
- It is a living guide: the 2.0 Revision launched in 2024 and a 3.0 evergreening initiative began in 2025 to modernize the framework ⟨dama.org⟩
- The framework is depicted as the DAMA Wheel, with Data Governance at the hub coordinating ten surrounding knowledge areas ⟨DAMA Wheel⟩
- Data Governance is positioned at the center because it provides authority, oversight, policy, and coordination across all other knowledge areas ⟨DAMA Wheel⟩
- Each knowledge area chapter follows a consistent context-diagram structure of goals, inputs, activities, deliverables, roles, tools/techniques, and metrics ⟨DAMA Wheel⟩
- Reference & Master Data combines Reference Data Management and Master Data Management as a single knowledge area on the Wheel ⟨DAMA Wheel⟩
- Data Warehousing & Business Intelligence covers the architecture and processes that turn integrated data into analytics and decision support ⟨DAMA Wheel⟩
- Metadata Management and Data Quality are treated as distinct knowledge areas underpinning trust and usability across the other functions ⟨DAMA Wheel⟩

# DCAM — Data Management Capability Assessment Model (EDM Council)

## Scope & purpose

> Note: The full DCAM framework is members-only (available exclusively to EDM Council / EDM Association member firms), so the model's detailed capabilities were not consulted. The key points below are drawn from the public DCAM overview page (edmcouncil.org) plus general knowledge of the DCAM framework; they are not quoted from the licensed DCAM specification.

DCAM (Data Management Capability Assessment Model) is the EDM Council's framework for assessing, benchmarking, and improving an organization's data management and analytics capabilities. It provides a structured set of capabilities and evaluation criteria that let an organization measure the maturity of its data program against industry best practices, identify gaps, build a business case, and define a roadmap. It is positioned as a descriptive best-practices reference rather than a regulation or a prescriptive standard, and it serves as the foundation of the EDM Council's Data Excellence Program.

## Structure

DCAM v3 is organized into eight key components that span the data supply chain, from establishing a data management foundation through managing advanced analytics. Beneath the components, the framework is constructed as a series of 34 required data management capabilities decomposed into 101 sub-capabilities. Each capability is expressed through objectives, questions, artifacts of evidence, and criteria for scoring, and progress is measured with a scoring matrix that evaluates engagement and processes against auditable evidence. Version 3 integrates an Architecture component into the eight and adds a new Business Data Knowledge component.

- It measures data management capabilities and progress using a scoring matrix that evaluates engagement and processes with auditable evidence ⟨edmcouncil.org/dcam⟩
- v3 brings heightened focus to data governance, privacy, and protection, and to aligning a data program with the latest regulations and compliance requirements ⟨edmcouncil.org/dcam⟩
- v3 enhances the eight components with an integrated Architecture component and a new component for Business Data Knowledge ⟨edmcouncil.org/dcam⟩
- v3 adds expanded support for AI/ML integration, cloud-native architectures, and modern data pipelines ⟨edmcouncil.org/dcam⟩
- Applications of DCAM include program initiation & funding, team training & compliance, establishing a common language, assessments & benchmarking, strategic roadmap, and compliance ⟨edmcouncil.org/dcam⟩
- It supports an objective self-assessment or assessment with a DCAM Authorized Partner to identify gaps and benchmark against peers ⟨edmcouncil.org/dcam⟩
- It is the foundation of the EDM Council's Data Excellence Program for ongoing achievement and recognition ⟨edmcouncil.org/dcam⟩
- It helps meet regulatory and compliance reporting requirements such as BCBS 239 and GDPR ⟨edmcouncil.org/dcam⟩
- DCAM is developed and maintained via collaboration of EDM Council (EDM Association) members and is available exclusively to member firms ⟨edmcouncil.org/dcam⟩

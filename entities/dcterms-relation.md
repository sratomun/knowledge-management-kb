# dcterms:relation

## What it is
A core Dublin Core element pointing to a related resource; the parent property of many relation subproperties.

## Key facts
- URI `http://purl.org/dc/terms/relation`; definition "A related resource." ⟨§2⟩
- Recommended practice is to identify the related resource by means of a URI. ⟨§2⟩
- Subproperty of `dc:relation`; parent of subproperties such as hasPart, isPartOf, references, replaces, conformsTo, and source. ⟨§2⟩

# prov:Bundle

## What it is
An Expanded term: a subclass of prov:Entity that is a named set of provenance descriptions, enabling provenance of provenance.

## Key facts
- "A prov:Bundle is a named set of provenance descriptions, which may itself have provenance." ⟨§3.2⟩
- "a Bundle of PROV-O assertions is an abstract set of RDF triples, and adding or removing a triple creates a new distinct Bundle of PROV-O assertions." ⟨§3.2⟩
- "Because a bundle is a kind of entity, all provenance assertions that can be made about entities can also be made about bundles. The use of bundles enables the creation of provenance of provenance." ⟨§3.2⟩

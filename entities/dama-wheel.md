# The DAMA Wheel

## What it is
The signature diagram of the DAMA-DMBOK2 that organizes the discipline of data management into eleven knowledge areas, with Data Governance at the hub coordinating the ten surrounding areas.

## Key facts
- Places Data Governance at the hub, providing authority and oversight across the ten surrounding knowledge areas [gen] ⟨DAMA Wheel⟩
- Surrounds the hub with Data Architecture, Data Modeling & Design, Data Storage & Operations, Data Security, Data Integration & Interoperability, Document & Content Management, Reference & Master Data, Data Warehousing & BI, Metadata Management, and Data Quality [gen] ⟨DAMA Wheel⟩
- Describes each knowledge area with a consistent context diagram of goals, activities, deliverables, roles, and metrics [gen] ⟨DAMA Wheel⟩
- Serves as the globally recognized structure of the DAMA-DMBOK framework published by DAMA International ⟨dama.org⟩

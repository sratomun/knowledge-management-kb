# FAIR F4

## What it is
A Findability sub-principle of the FAIR Guiding Principles: data and metadata must be registered or indexed in a searchable resource (the infrastructure component) so they can be discovered by humans and machines.

## Key facts
- (Meta)data are registered or indexed in a searchable resource ⟨F4⟩

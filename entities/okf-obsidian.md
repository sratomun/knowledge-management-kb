# Obsidian

## What it is

Obsidian is a markdown-vault knowledge tool cited by OKF as a familiar precedent for the LLM-wiki pattern and as one bespoke instance of the knowledge-as-wiki approach the format aims to make interoperable.

## Key facts

- Named among tools whose "shape will feel familiar" — "If you've used Obsidian, Notion, Hugo, or any of the LLM wiki patterns that have emerged over the past year" ⟨Introducing the Open Knowledge Format⟩
- Cited as a recurring bespoke instance of the pattern: "Obsidian vaults wired to coding agents" ⟨Knowledge as a living wiki⟩
- Such instances "may all look alike (markdown, frontmatter, cross-links)" but "none of them are intentionally designed to cooperate" ⟨Knowledge as a living wiki⟩

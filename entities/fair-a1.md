# FAIR A1

## What it is
The first Accessibility sub-principle of the FAIR Guiding Principles: once found, data and metadata must be retrievable by their identifier using a standardised communications protocol.

## Key facts
- (Meta)data are retrievable by their identifier using a standardised communications protocol ⟨A1⟩

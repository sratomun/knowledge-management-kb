# ISO 15836-1:2017

## What it is
The ISO standardization of the fifteen-element Dublin Core, corresponding to the scope of the DCMI /elements/1.1/ namespace.

## Key facts
- The /elements/1.1/ namespace corresponds to the original scope of ISO 15836, which was published first in 2003 and last revised in 2017 as ISO 15836-1:2017. ⟨§1⟩
- Reference URL `https://www.iso.org/standard/71339.html`. ⟨§1 References⟩
- Published by ISO. ⟨§1⟩

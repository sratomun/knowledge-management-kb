# W3C Knowledge Graph Construction Community Group

## What it is
The W3C Knowledge Graph Construction Community Group is the community group that maintains and develops the RML (RDF Mapping Language) specification.

## Key facts
- Maintains RML and is developing a newer version of the specification at https://w3id.org/rml/ ⟨rml⟩
- RML is published as a W3C Community Group product ("Unofficial Draft") that has no official standing and is not a W3C Recommendation ⟨rml⟩

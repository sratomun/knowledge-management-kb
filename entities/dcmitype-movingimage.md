# DCMIType MovingImage

## What it is
A DCMI Type Vocabulary class for a series of visual representations imparting an impression of motion when shown in succession.

## Key facts
- URI `http://purl.org/dc/dcmitype/MovingImage`; definition "A series of visual representations imparting an impression of motion when shown in succession." ⟨§7⟩
- Comment: examples include animations, movies, television programs, videos; instances must also be describable as instances of the broader type Image. ⟨§7⟩
- Member Of `DCMIType`; Subclass of `dcmitype:Image`. ⟨§7⟩

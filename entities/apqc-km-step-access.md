# Access (APQC KM Framework)

## What it is
The step of the APQC knowledge flow process in which people can find and retrieve the knowledge they need when they need it.

## Key facts
- Access is the retrieval step preceding Use/Apply in the seven-step knowledge flow [gen] ⟨APQC KM Framework⟩
- Getting knowledge to flow to and between the right people at the right time depends on people being able to access it ⟨apqc.org/expertise/knowledge-management⟩

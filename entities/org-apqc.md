# APQC

## What it is
APQC (the American Productivity & Quality Center) is the member-based nonprofit that publishes the Process Classification Framework (PCF) and related benchmarking and knowledge-management resources.

## Key facts
- Developed the Process Classification Framework (PCF) in 1992, described as the world's most widely used business process framework ⟨apqc-pcf⟩
- Publishes both a cross-industry PCF and industry-specific PCF variants, authoring the process definitions and recommended KPIs tied to each ⟨apqc-pcf⟩

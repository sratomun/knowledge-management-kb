# ISO/IEC 11179-2 — Classification

## What it is
Part 2 (Classification) covers the classification of registry content (administered items) using classification schemes.

## Key facts
- A data element may be classified in a classification scheme per Part 2 ⟨Wikipedia: ISO/IEC 11179⟩
- Part 2 is subsumed by the Concept Region introduced in Part 3 and is being updated to a Technical Report giving guidance on developing classification schemes ⟨Wikipedia: ISO/IEC 11179⟩
- Published as ISO/IEC 11179-2:2005 ⟨Wikipedia: ISO/IEC 11179⟩

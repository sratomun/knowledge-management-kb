# TGN (Getty Thesaurus of Geographic Names VES)

## What it is
A DCMI vocabulary encoding scheme naming the Getty Thesaurus of Geographic Names as a source of place values.

## Key facts
- URI `http://purl.org/dc/terms/TGN`; Label "TGN". ⟨§4⟩
- Definition "The set of places specified by the Getty Thesaurus of Geographic Names." ⟨§4⟩
- Recommended by dcterms:coverage as a controlled vocabulary for named places. ⟨§2⟩

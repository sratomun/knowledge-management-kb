# IBM Research

## What it is
IBM Research (the AI for knowledge / Deep Search team, Zurich) is the group that develops Docling, an open-source document-conversion toolkit, and its underlying layout and table-structure models.

## Key facts
- Released Docling, an MIT-licensed open-source package for PDF document conversion, hosted in the LF AI & Data Foundation ⟨docling⟩
- Developed the layout-analysis model (DocLayNet-trained) and TableFormer table-structure model that power Docling ⟨docling⟩

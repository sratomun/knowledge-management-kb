# ISO/TC 260

## What it is
ISO/TC 260 is the ISO technical committee for Human Resource Management, the committee under which the ISO 30401 knowledge-management-systems standard was developed.

## Key facts
- ISO 30401:2018 (Knowledge management systems — Requirements), a certifiable management-system standard following the ISO Harmonized Structure, was developed under ISO/TC 260 [gen] ⟨iso-30401⟩

# W3C Provenance Working Group

## What it is
The W3C Provenance Working Group is the working group that produced the PROV family of provenance standards, including PROV-O.

## Key facts
- Produced PROV-O, the W3C Recommendation of 30 April 2013 that expresses the PROV Data Model in OWL2, forming a provenance-interchange framework together with PROV-AQ and PROV-DM ⟨prov-o⟩

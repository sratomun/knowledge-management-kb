# The Open Group

## What it is
The Open Group is the standards body that publishes and maintains the TOGAF Standard, a widely used Enterprise Architecture methodology and framework.

## Key facts
- Publishes the TOGAF Standard, a proven Enterprise Architecture methodology and framework used by leading organizations to improve business efficiency ⟨opengroup.org/togaf⟩
- Maintains the standard and its certification portfolio, and publishes the related ArchiMate Specification, a separate modeling language for Enterprise Architecture [gen] ⟨TOGAF⟩

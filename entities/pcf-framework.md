# PCF (framework)

## What it is
The APQC Process Classification Framework (PCF) is a taxonomy of business processes — a
hierarchically grouped list of the key processes an organization performs — that lets
organizations objectively track and compare their performance internally and externally
against organizations from any industry.

## Key facts
- The PCF is described as "a taxonomy of business processes" allowing objective internal and external performance comparison across industries ⟨apqc.org/process-frameworks⟩
- Developed in 1992, the PCF is presented as the world's most widely used business process framework ⟨apqc.org/process-frameworks⟩
- It is used by hundreds of leading companies worldwide as a reliable and robust reference ⟨apqc.org/process-frameworks⟩
- The PCF creates a common language so organizations can improve process management ⟨apqc.org/process-frameworks⟩
- APQC identifies three ways organizations use the PCF to create business value: benchmarking, content management, and process management ⟨apqc.org/process-frameworks⟩
- The PCF forms the basis for a variety of projects related to business processes ⟨apqc.org/process-frameworks⟩

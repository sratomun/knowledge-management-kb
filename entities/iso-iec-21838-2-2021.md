# ISO/IEC 21838-2:2021

## What it is

ISO/IEC 21838-2:2021 is the international-standard edition that specifies Basic Formal Ontology (BFO) as a top-level ontology. It is Part 2 of the multi-part ISO/IEC 21838 standard "Information technology — Top-level ontologies (TLO)", giving BFO 2020 the status of a standardized, domain-neutral upper ontology usable as an interoperability backbone for domain ontologies.

## Key facts

- The normative BFO version is BFO 2020, aligned with the ISO/IEC 21838-2:2021 standard, with sources maintained under the BFO-2020 project ⟨basic-formal-ontology.org / Home → GitHub github.com/bfo-ontology/BFO-2020⟩.
- ISO/IEC 21838-2 standardizes BFO itself, while ISO/IEC 21838-1 specifies the requirements a top-level ontology must satisfy [gen] ⟨basic-formal-ontology.org / Home⟩.
- The standard is published jointly by [[org-iso]] and [[org-iec]] as a joint ISO/IEC deliverable [gen] ⟨basic-formal-ontology.org / Home⟩.
- BFO as standardized is "a small, upper level ontology" designed to support information retrieval, analysis and integration in scientific and other domains ⟨basic-formal-ontology.org / Home⟩.
- The standardized ontology is "used by more than 550 ontology-driven endeavors throughout the world" ⟨basic-formal-ontology.org / Home⟩.

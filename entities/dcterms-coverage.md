# dcterms:coverage

## What it is
A core Dublin Core element for the spatial/temporal topic, spatial applicability, or jurisdiction of the resource.

## Key facts
- URI `http://purl.org/dc/terms/coverage`; definition "The spatial or temporal topic of the resource, spatial applicability of the resource, or jurisdiction under which the resource is relevant." ⟨§2⟩
- Recommended practice is to use a controlled vocabulary such as the Getty Thesaurus of Geographic Names [TGN]; it is preferable to use the more specific subproperties Temporal Coverage and Spatial Coverage. ⟨§2⟩
- Range Includes `Location`, `Period`, `Jurisdiction`; subproperty of `dc:coverage`. ⟨§2⟩

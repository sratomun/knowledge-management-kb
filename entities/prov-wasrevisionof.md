# prov:wasRevisionOf

## What it is
An Expanded term: a subproperty of prov:wasDerivedFrom for revision derivations.

## Key facts
- "prov:wasRevisionOf indicates that the derived Entity contains substantial content from the original Entity (e.g., two editions of a book)." ⟨§3.2⟩
- It is one of three subproperties of prov:wasDerivedFrom provided for certain kinds of derivation among Entities. ⟨§3.2⟩
- Can be qualified with prov:qualifiedRevision and the class prov:Revision. ⟨§3.3 Table 3⟩

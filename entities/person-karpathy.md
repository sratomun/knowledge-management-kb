# Andrej Karpathy

## What it is
Andrej Karpathy is the AI practitioner whose "LLM Wiki" gist articulated the living-wiki pattern that the Open Knowledge Format formalizes.

## Key facts
- His LLM Wiki gist frames the idea that "LLMs don't get bored, don't forget to update a cross-reference, and can touch 15 files in one pass," cited by OKF as articulating the living-wiki pattern ⟨okf⟩

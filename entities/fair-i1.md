# FAIR I1

## What it is
The first Interoperability sub-principle of the FAIR Guiding Principles: data and metadata should use a formal, accessible, shared, and broadly applicable language for knowledge representation.

## Key facts
- (Meta)data use a formal, accessible, shared, and broadly applicable language for knowledge representation. ⟨I1⟩

# Wikimedia Deutschland

## What it is
Wikimedia Deutschland is the non-profit that makes and supports Wikibase, the open-source software suite for building collaborative, structured knowledge bases published as Linked Open Data.

## Key facts
- Makes and supports Wikibase, a free, open-source software suite for creating collaborative, structured knowledge bases and opening them to the Linked Open Data web ⟨wikibase: homepage⟩
- Originally developed Wikibase to power Wikidata and hosts Wikibase Cloud, a SaaS instance of the software ⟨wikibase: homepage/Showcases — Wikidata⟩

# FAIR A1.1

## What it is
An Accessibility sub-principle of the FAIR Guiding Principles refining A1: the communications protocol used to retrieve (meta)data must be open, free, and universally implementable.

## Key facts
- The protocol is open, free, and universally implementable ⟨A1.1⟩

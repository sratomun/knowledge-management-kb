# Consortium for Service Innovation

## What it is
The Consortium for Service Innovation is the member organization whose members created and maintain the Knowledge-Centered Success (KCS) methodology; it is the only certifying body for KCS.

## Key facts
- Created and maintains the KCS methodology and its open-source Practices Guide and Adoption Guide ⟨serviceinnovation.org/kcs⟩
- Is the only certifying body for KCS, offering certification for people and designations for KCS Verified/Aligned tools and services ⟨serviceinnovation.org/kcs⟩

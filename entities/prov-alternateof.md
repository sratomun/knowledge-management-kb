# prov:alternateOf

## What it is
An Expanded term relating Entities that present aspects of the same thing.

## Key facts
- "prov:alternateOf links Entities that present aspects of the same thing, but not necessarily the same aspects or at the same time (e.g., the serialization of a document in different formats or a backup copy of a computer file)." ⟨§3.2⟩
- It belongs to the category of expanded terms that relates Entities according to their levels of abstraction. ⟨§3.2⟩

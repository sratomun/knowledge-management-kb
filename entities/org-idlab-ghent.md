# IDLab, Ghent University

## What it is
IDLab at Ghent University is the research lab associated with the development of RML; the original RML vocabulary namespace is hosted at semweb.mmlab.be (Ghent).

## Key facts
- The RML vocabulary namespace is http://semweb.mmlab.be/ns/rml#, hosted at the Ghent research lab that originated RML [gen] ⟨rml⟩

# ISO

## What it is
The International Organization for Standardization (ISO) is the international standards body that publishes International Standards across domains; in this corpus it publishes the ISO 15836 (Dublin Core) family, and co-publishes ISO/IEC 11179 and ISO 30401.

## Key facts
- The legacy fifteen-element Dublin Core corresponds to the original scope of ISO 15836, revised in 2017 as ISO 15836-1:2017, with the most useful terms published as ISO 15836-2:2019 ⟨dcmi-terms⟩

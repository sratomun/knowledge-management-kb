# FAIR R1.2

## What it is
A Reusability sub-principle of the FAIR Guiding Principles refining R1: data and metadata should be associated with detailed provenance describing their origin and processing history.

## Key facts
- (Meta)data are associated with detailed provenance ⟨R1.2⟩

# Publications Office of the European Union

## What it is
The Publications Office of the European Union manages EUR-Lex and stewards the European Legislation Identifier (ELI) standard for identifying and describing legislation across Europe.

## Key facts
- Manages the ELI register and the EUR-Lex platform on which ELI is implemented ⟨eli⟩

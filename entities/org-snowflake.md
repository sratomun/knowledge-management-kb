# Snowflake

## What it is
Snowflake is the data-cloud company that announced and leads the Open Semantic Interchange (OSI), a collaborative open-source initiative to standardize the exchange of semantic-layer models.

## Key facts
- Announced OSI on Sep 23, 2025 to define a vendor-neutral open standard for exchanging semantic-layer models across data analytics, AI, and BI tools ⟨osi: announce §A Common Semantic Standard⟩
- Framed OSI as solving a foundational AI challenge — the lack of a common semantic standard — with the industry "coming together, not competing" ⟨osi: announce §intro / Kleinerman quote⟩

# DCAM — Required Capability

## What it is
The unit of structure beneath DCAM's eight components: a required data management capability, further decomposed into sub-capabilities, each carrying the objectives and scoring criteria an assessment evaluates.

## Key facts
- DCAM v3 is constructed as 34 required data management capabilities with 101 sub-capabilities ⟨edmcouncil.org/dcam⟩
- Each capability spans objectives, questions, artifacts of evidence, and criteria for scoring ⟨edmcouncil.org/dcam⟩
- The eight key components group these capabilities across the data supply chain, from establishing a data management foundation through managing advanced analytics ⟨edmcouncil.org/dcam⟩
- [gen] v3 adds new capabilities and sub-capabilities while consolidating others for a more streamlined framework ⟨edmcouncil.org/dcam⟩

# rdfs:label

## What it is
`rdfs:label` is the property used to provide a human-readable version of a resource's name.

## Key facts
- "rdfs:label is an instance of rdf:Property that may be used to provide a human-readable version of a resource's name." ⟨§3.6⟩
- The `rdfs:domain` of `rdfs:label` is `rdfs:Resource` and its `rdfs:range` is `rdfs:Literal` ⟨§3.6⟩.
- "Multilingual labels are supported using the language tagging facility of RDF literals." ⟨§3.6⟩

# dbt Labs

## What it is
dbt Labs is the vendor behind dbt and the dbt Semantic Layer, which lets data teams define business metrics centrally in the modeling layer rather than in individual BI tools.

## Key facts
- Develops and maintains the dbt Semantic Layer and MetricFlow, distributing MetricFlow under the Apache 2.0 license ⟨dbt: about-metricflow/MetricFlow⟩
- The dbt Semantic Layer centralizes metric definitions in the modeling layer so a definition changed in dbt is refreshed everywhere it is invoked, giving downstream tools consistent self-service access ⟨dbt: dbt-sl/overview⟩

# NIST

## What it is
The U.S. National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) is a federal agency that develops measurement standards and voluntary frameworks; in this corpus it authors the AI Risk Management Framework.

## Key facts
- Published the AI Risk Management Framework 1.0 (NIST AI 100-1) as voluntary, sector-agnostic guidance for trustworthy AI [gen] ⟨nist-ai-rmf⟩

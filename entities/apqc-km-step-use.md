# Use (APQC KM Framework)

## What it is
The step of the APQC knowledge flow process (also framed as Use/Apply) in which knowledge is applied in the flow of work to produce business outcomes, which in turn generates new knowledge.

## Key facts
- Use/Apply closes the loop, generating new knowledge that feeds back into Create [gen] ⟨APQC KM Framework⟩
- Applying knowledge in the flow of work is where KM drives measurable business impact ⟨apqc.org/expertise/knowledge-management⟩

# bfo:DependentContinuant

## What it is

A dependent continuant is a subcategory of the BFO continuant: an entity that persists through time but depends on other entities for its existence. It splits into specifically dependent continuants (qualities, roles, dispositions and functions that inhere in a bearer) and generically dependent continuants (such as information artifacts that can be copied across bearers).

## Key facts

- Dependent continuant is one of the subdivisions of the BFO continuant category [gen] ⟨basic-formal-ontology.org / Home⟩.
- Specifically dependent continuants include qualities, roles, dispositions and functions [gen] ⟨basic-formal-ontology.org / Home⟩.
- Generically dependent continuants include information artifacts [gen] ⟨basic-formal-ontology.org / Home⟩.
- Specifically dependent continuants depend on independent continuants as their bearers [gen] ⟨basic-formal-ontology.org / Home⟩.
- The Information Artifact Ontology (IAO), which models generically dependent information entities, is listed among BFO users ⟨basic-formal-ontology.org / users.html⟩.

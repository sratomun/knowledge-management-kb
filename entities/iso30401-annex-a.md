# ISO 30401 Annex A (knowledge spectrum)

## What it is
An informative annex of ISO 30401:2018 that describes the knowledge spectrum — the range spanning data and information through explicit (codified) and tacit (experiential) knowledge that a knowledge management system is intended to address.

## Key facts
- Describes the knowledge spectrum from data/information through explicit and tacit knowledge. ⟨iso.org/standard/68683⟩ [gen]
- Is informative (guidance), not part of the auditable requirement clauses. ⟨iso.org/standard/68683⟩ [gen]

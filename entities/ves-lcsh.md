# LCSH (Library of Congress Subject Headings VES)

## What it is
A DCMI vocabulary encoding scheme naming the Library of Congress Subject Headings as a source of values.

## Key facts
- URI `http://purl.org/dc/terms/LCSH`; Label "LCSH". ⟨§4⟩
- Definition "The set of labeled concepts specified by the Library of Congress Subject Headings." ⟨§4⟩
- Named as a controlled vocabulary suitable for subject values. ⟨§4⟩

# Identify (APQC KM Framework)

## What it is
The step of the APQC knowledge flow process in which critical knowledge — what must be known, and by whom — is recognized and prioritized, often through knowledge mapping.

## Key facts
- APQC calls out identifying and mapping critical knowledge as a distinct capability ⟨apqc.org/expertise/knowledge-management⟩
- Identification addresses what knowledge is at risk (e.g., from retirements) and who holds it ⟨apqc.org/expertise/knowledge-management⟩
- Identify precedes Collect in the seven-step knowledge flow [gen] ⟨APQC KM Framework⟩

# Karpathy LLM Wiki gist

## What it is

The LLM Wiki gist by Andrej Karpathy is the articulation OKF credits with crystallizing the knowledge-as-living-wiki idea that the format formalizes.

## Key facts

- Andrej Karpathy "articulates this idea most crisply in his LLM Wiki gist" ⟨Knowledge as a living wiki⟩
- Karpathy writes: "LLMs don't get bored, don't forget to update a cross-reference, and can touch 15 files in one pass" ⟨Knowledge as a living wiki⟩
- The gist observes that "the bookkeeping that causes humans to abandon personal wikis is exactly what LLMs are good at" ⟨Knowledge as a living wiki⟩

# Share (APQC KM Framework)

## What it is
The step of the APQC knowledge flow process in which knowledge is moved and transferred between people, teams, and systems — for example through communities of practice and knowledge transfer methods.

## Key facts
- Knowledge sharing is the practice of moving useful know-how from people, teams, and systems to the people who need it, and includes more than publishing documents ⟨apqc.org/expertise/knowledge-management⟩
- Communities of practice are a core KM approach that enables knowledge sharing and reuse ⟨apqc.org/expertise/knowledge-management⟩
- Sharing supports continuity across retirements, turnover, and role changes ⟨apqc.org/expertise/knowledge-management⟩

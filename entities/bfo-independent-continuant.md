# bfo:IndependentContinuant

## What it is

An independent continuant is a subcategory of the BFO continuant: an entity that persists through time and does not depend for its existence on any other entity as its bearer. It covers objects, their parts and their aggregates — the material "things" that serve as bearers for the qualities, roles and dispositions modeled as dependent continuants.

## Key facts

- Independent continuant is one of the subdivisions of the BFO continuant category [gen] ⟨basic-formal-ontology.org / Home⟩.
- Independent continuants include objects and their parts and aggregates [gen] ⟨basic-formal-ontology.org / Home⟩.
- Independent continuants act as the bearers on which specifically dependent continuants (qualities, roles, dispositions, functions) depend [gen] ⟨basic-formal-ontology.org / Home⟩.
- As a genuine upper-ontology category, independent continuant contains no physical, chemical or biological terms proper to the special sciences ⟨basic-formal-ontology.org / Home⟩.
- The category is fixed in the normative BFO 2020 release maintained under the BFO-2020 project ⟨basic-formal-ontology.org / Home → GitHub github.com/bfo-ontology/BFO-2020⟩.

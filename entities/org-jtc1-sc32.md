# ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 32

## What it is
ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 32 is the subcommittee for Data management and interchange within the ISO/IEC Joint Technical Committee 1, responsible for developing and maintaining data-management standards including ISO/IEC 11179.

## Key facts
- ISO/IEC 11179 is developed and maintained under ISO/IEC JTC 1/SC 32 (Data management and interchange) ⟨iso-iec-11179-1⟩

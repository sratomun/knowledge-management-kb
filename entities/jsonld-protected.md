# @protected

## What it is
The keyword that prevents term definitions in a context from being overridden by subsequent contexts.

## Key facts
- The unaliased @protected keyword MAY be used in a context definition, or an expanded term definition; its value MUST be true or false ⟨§9.16⟩.
- It is used to prevent term definitions of a context from being overridden by other contexts ⟨§1.7⟩ ⟨§4.1.11⟩.

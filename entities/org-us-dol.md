# U.S. Department of Labor / National Center for O*NET Development

## What it is
The U.S. Department of Labor, through its Employment and Training Administration and the National Center for O*NET Development, sponsors and maintains the O*NET occupational information system.

## Key facts
- Sponsors O*NET, the primary U.S. source of occupational information, published openly as the O*NET database and O*NET OnLine [gen] ⟨onet⟩

# PCF Task (Level 5)

## What it is
A Task is the fifth and most granular level of the PCF hierarchy — the finest-grained,
often organization-specific unit of work within an Activity.

## Key facts
- Task is Level 5, the lowest and most granular level of the PCF hierarchy, below Activity [gen] ⟨apqc.org/process-frameworks⟩

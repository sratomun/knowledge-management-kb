# skos:semanticRelation

## What it is
The super-property of all SKOS links between concepts whose link is inherent in the meaning of the linked concepts. It generalizes the hierarchical, associative and mapping relation properties.

## Key facts
- skos:semanticRelation is an instance of owl:ObjectProperty ⟨S18⟩.
- The rdfs:domain of skos:semanticRelation is the class skos:Concept ⟨S19⟩ and its rdfs:range is the class skos:Concept ⟨S20⟩.
- skos:broaderTransitive, skos:narrowerTransitive and skos:related are each sub-properties of skos:semanticRelation ⟨S21⟩.

# DCMI Period (SES)

## What it is
A DCMI syntax encoding scheme (datatype) for time intervals defined by their limits.

## Key facts
- URI `http://purl.org/dc/terms/Period`; Label "DCMI Period". ⟨§5⟩
- Definition "The set of time intervals defined by their limits according to the DCMI Period Encoding Scheme." ⟨§5⟩

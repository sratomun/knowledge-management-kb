# W3C

## What it is
The World Wide Web Consortium (W3C) is the international standards organization that develops and publishes Recommendations for the Web, including the RDF, SKOS, JSON-LD, PROV-O, and DCAT semantic-web vocabularies used across this corpus.

## Key facts
- Published RDF 1.1 Concepts and Abstract Syntax as a W3C Recommendation on 25 February 2014 ⟨rdf-11-concepts⟩
- Its Recommendations define terms that other specifications normatively reference; implementations conform to those referencing specs rather than to the Concepts document directly ⟨rdf-11-concepts⟩

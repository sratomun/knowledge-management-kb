# Semantic Arts

## What it is
Semantic Arts is the consultancy that develops and publishes gist, a minimalist upper (foundational) ontology for the enterprise, and markets it as the basis for enterprise knowledge graphs.

## Key facts
- Publishes gist, described as a minimalist upper ontology providing "maximum coverage of typical business concepts with the fewest number of primitives and the least amount of ambiguity" ⟨semanticarts.com/gist⟩
- Distributes gist free under CC BY 4.0 and offers industry extensions (cybersecurity, pharmaceuticals, accounting, HR, professional services) plus gistBFO, a bridge ontology aligning gist with the Basic Formal Ontology ⟨semanticarts.com/gist⟩

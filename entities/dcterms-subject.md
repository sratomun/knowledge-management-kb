# dcterms:subject

## What it is
A core Dublin Core element giving a topic of the resource.

## Key facts
- URI `http://purl.org/dc/terms/subject`; definition "A topic of the resource." ⟨§2⟩
- Recommended practice is to refer to the subject with a URI, preferably from a controlled vocabulary. ⟨§2⟩
- Subproperty of `dc:subject`. ⟨§2⟩

# DCAM — Data Governance component

## What it is
One of the DCAM framework's capability components, covering the authority, oversight, policy, and controls an organization applies to managing its data. Data governance is treated as a central, cross-cutting concern within DCAM.

## Key facts
- DCAM v3 brings heightened focus to data governance, privacy, and protection, and to aligning a data program with the latest regulations and compliance requirements ⟨edmcouncil.org/dcam⟩
- Case-study use frames DCAM as strengthening governance, data quality, architecture, and operating models in support of business growth and regulatory confidence ⟨edmcouncil.org/dcam⟩
- [gen] As a capability assessment model, DCAM positions data governance as a central, cross-cutting capability rather than a siloed function ⟨DCAM⟩
- [gen] Earlier public DCAM materials framed Data Governance as one of the model's named components ⟨DCAM⟩

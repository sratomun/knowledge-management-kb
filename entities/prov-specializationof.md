# prov:specializationOf

## What it is
An Expanded term relating Entities by level of abstraction, linking a more specific Entity to a more general one.

## Key facts
- "prov:specializationOf links a more specific Entity to a more general one (e.g., today's BBC news home page versus BBC's news home page on any day)." ⟨§3.2⟩
- It belongs to the category of expanded terms that "relates Entities according to their levels of abstraction, where some Entities may present more specific aspects than their more general counterparts." ⟨§3.2⟩

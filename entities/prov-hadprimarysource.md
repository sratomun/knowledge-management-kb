# prov:hadPrimarySource

## What it is
An Expanded term: a subproperty of prov:wasDerivedFrom for primary-source derivations.

## Key facts
- "prov:hadPrimarySource cites a preceding Entity produced by some agent with direct experience and knowledge about the topic (such as a reading from a sensor, or a journal written during an historical event)." ⟨§3.2⟩
- It is one of three subproperties of prov:wasDerivedFrom provided for certain kinds of derivation among Entities. ⟨§3.2⟩
- Can be qualified with prov:qualifiedPrimarySource and the class prov:PrimarySource. ⟨§3.3 Table 3⟩

# Microsoft Research

## What it is
Microsoft Research is the research organization that introduced the GraphRAG method and released its open-source implementation.

## Key facts
- Authored the "From Local to Global" GraphRAG approach to query-focused summarization over private text corpora ⟨graphrag-local-to-global⟩
- Released the open-source GraphRAG data pipeline and transformation suite (microsoft/graphrag) ⟨ms-graphrag-implementation⟩

# PCF Category (Level 1)

## What it is
A Category is the highest level of the PCF hierarchy — the framework's top-level grouping
of processes, under which related process groups are organized.

## Key facts
- Each process category has its own document with detailed definitions and key measures tying to the processes it contains ⟨apqc.org/process-frameworks⟩
- The cross-industry PCF is conventionally organized around 13 top-level categories spanning operating and management-and-support processes [gen] ⟨apqc.org/process-frameworks⟩
- Category is the broadest level (Level 1), sitting above Process Group in the five-level hierarchy [gen] ⟨apqc.org/process-frameworks⟩

# ISO 30401 Annex C (knowledge management culture)

## What it is
An informative annex of ISO 30401:2018 addressing knowledge management culture — the organizational culture and behaviours needed to support an effective knowledge management system.

## Key facts
- Addresses the organizational culture needed to support a knowledge management system. ⟨iso.org/standard/68683⟩ [gen]
- Is informative (guidance), not part of the auditable requirement clauses. ⟨iso.org/standard/68683⟩ [gen]

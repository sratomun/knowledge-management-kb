# DCMI

## What it is
The Dublin Core Metadata Initiative (DCMI) is the organization that maintains the DCMI Metadata Terms (Dublin Core) vocabulary of descriptive-metadata properties, classes, and encoding schemes.

## Key facts
- Maintains all DCMI metadata terms — the fifteen-element Dublin Core plus several dozen extension properties, classes, datatypes, and vocabulary encoding schemes ⟨dcmi-terms⟩
- Gently encourages use of the /terms/ namespace over the legacy /elements/1.1/ namespace, while committing to support the latter indefinitely ⟨dcmi-terms⟩

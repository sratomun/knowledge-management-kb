# DCMI /elements/1.1/ namespace

## What it is
The DCMI namespace holding the original fifteen-element Dublin Core, widely used in data for more than twenty years.

## Key facts
- Base URI `http://purl.org/dc/elements/1.1/`; created in 2000 for the RDF representation of the fifteen-element Dublin Core. ⟨§1⟩
- Corresponds to the original scope of ISO 15836, published first in 2003 and last revised in 2017 as ISO 15836-1:2017. ⟨§1⟩
- The fifteen elements here have no formal ranges; DCMI will support this namespace indefinitely but gently encourages use of the /terms/ namespace. ⟨§1⟩

# SECI model

## What it is
The SECI model (Socialization, Externalization, Combination, Internalization) is a model of organizational knowledge creation and conveyance, describing how tacit and explicit knowledge are converted through four modes. It underpins the knowledge-conveyance ideas associated with ISO 30401's knowledge management system.

## Key facts
- Comprises four knowledge-conversion modes: Socialization, Externalization, Combination, and Internalization. ⟨iso.org/standard/68683⟩ [gen]
- Provides the knowledge-creation/conveyance basis for how a KMS moves knowledge between tacit and explicit forms. ⟨iso.org/standard/68683⟩ [gen]
- Originates in Nonaka & Takeuchi's account of organizational knowledge creation as a continuous tacit↔explicit spiral. ⟨Nonaka & Takeuchi 1995, SECI⟩ [gen]

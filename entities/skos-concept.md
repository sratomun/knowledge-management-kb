# skos:Concept

## What it is
The class of SKOS concepts — a concept being an idea or notion, a unit of thought — used to describe the conceptual/intellectual structure of a knowledge organization system. The definition is meant to be suggestive rather than restrictive.

## Key facts
- skos:Concept is an instance of owl:Class ⟨S1⟩.
- A SKOS concept "can be viewed as an idea or notion; a unit of thought," but what constitutes a unit of thought is subjective ⟨§3.1⟩.
- This specification makes no additional statement about the formal relationship between the class of SKOS concepts and the classes of OWL classes or OWL properties, leaving applications free to explore design patterns ⟨§3.5.1⟩.

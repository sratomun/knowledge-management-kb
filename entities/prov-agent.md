# prov:Agent

## What it is
One of the three Starting Point classes of PROV-O. An agent bears some form of responsibility for an activity, an entity, or another agent's activity.

## Key facts
- "An prov:Agent is something that bears some form of responsibility for an activity taking place, for the existence of an entity, or for another agent's activity." ⟨§3.1⟩
- "Three subclasses of Agent (prov:Person, prov:Organization, and prov:SoftwareAgent)" are provided. ⟨§3.2⟩
- "Agents can also be responsible for other Agents' actions. In this case of delegation, the influencing Agent prov:actedOnBehalfOf another Agent." ⟨§3.1⟩

# FAIR F1

## What it is
The first Findability sub-principle of the FAIR Guiding Principles: it requires that data and metadata carry a globally unique and persistent identifier so they can be unambiguously referenced and located.

## Key facts
- (Meta)data are assigned a globally unique and persistent identifier ⟨F1⟩

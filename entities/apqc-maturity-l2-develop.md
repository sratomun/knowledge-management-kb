# APQC KM Maturity — Level 2: Develop

## What it is
The second level of APQC's Levels of Knowledge Management Maturity, in which the organization begins building an intentional KM strategy and piloting approaches. [gen] The public page did not enumerate the levels; this description is general knowledge of APQC's model, not the gated resource text.

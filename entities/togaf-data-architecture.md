# TOGAF Data Architecture

## What it is

Data Architecture is one of the four TOGAF architecture domains; it describes the structure of an organization's logical and physical data assets and data management resources. [gen]

## Key facts

- Data Architecture is one of the four architecture domains of the TOGAF Standard. [gen] ⟨TOGAF⟩
- It describes the structure of an organization's logical and physical data assets and its data management resources. [gen] ⟨TOGAF⟩
- Together with Application Architecture it forms the Information Systems Architectures developed in Phase C of the ADM. [gen] ⟨TOGAF⟩
- The four architecture domains are commonly treated as a subset of the overall enterprise architecture the TOGAF Standard supports. ⟨opengroup.org/togaf⟩

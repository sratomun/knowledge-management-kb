# TOGAF Application Architecture

## What it is

Application Architecture is one of the four TOGAF architecture domains; it provides a blueprint for the individual application systems to be deployed, their interactions, and their relationships to the enterprise's core business processes. [gen]

## Key facts

- Application Architecture is one of the four architecture domains of the TOGAF Standard. [gen] ⟨TOGAF⟩
- It provides a blueprint for the individual application systems to be deployed, their interactions, and their relationships to core business processes. [gen] ⟨TOGAF⟩
- Together with Data Architecture it forms the Information Systems Architectures developed in Phase C of the ADM. [gen] ⟨TOGAF⟩
- The four architecture domains are commonly treated as a subset of the overall enterprise architecture the TOGAF Standard supports. ⟨opengroup.org/togaf⟩

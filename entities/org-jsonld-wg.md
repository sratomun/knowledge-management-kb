# W3C JSON-LD Working Group

## What it is
The W3C JSON-LD Working Group is the working group that produced the three JSON-LD 1.1 Recommendations, including the JSON-LD 1.1 syntax document.

## Key facts
- Produced JSON-LD 1.1, a W3C Recommendation of 16 July 2020, one of its three JSON-LD 1.1 Recommendations (Syntax, Processing Algorithms and API, and Framing) ⟨json-ld-11⟩

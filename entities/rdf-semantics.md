# RDF 1.1 Semantics

## What it is
RDF 1.1 Semantics is the companion specification that defines the formal model-theoretic meaning of RDF triples and graphs, and the entailment regimes built on it.

## Key facts
- The precise details of the meaning of RDF triples and graphs are the subject of the RDF Semantics specification, which yields the entailment, equivalence, and inconsistency relationships between RDF graphs ⟨§1.7⟩.
- An entailment regime is a specification that defines precise conditions that make these relationships hold ⟨§1.7⟩.
- The term "resource" is synonymous with "entity" as it is used in the RDF Semantics specification ⟨§1.2⟩.

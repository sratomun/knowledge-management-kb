# KCS methodology

## What it is
Knowledge-Centered Success (KCS) is a methodology, created and maintained by the Consortium for Service Innovation, for capturing and reusing an organization's collective experience in real time — creating and improving knowledge as a by-product of solving issues rather than as a separate activity.

## Key facts
- The KCS source describes it as a methodology to leverage an organization's collective experience in real time, keeping content accurate, structured, and trusted ⟨serviceinnovation.org/kcs⟩
- KCS is organized as a **double-loop model**: the Solve Loop (individual, real-time, in the workflow of solving user issues) and the Evolve Loop (collective, periodic, improving the knowledge base and organization) ⟨KCS v6 Practices Guide⟩ [gen]
- Solve Loop practices are Capture, Structure, Reuse, and Improve — creating knowledge as a by-product of solving and modifying articles in the moment of use ⟨KCS v6 Practices Guide⟩ [gen]
- Evolve Loop practices are Content Health, Process Integration, Performance Assessment, and Leadership & Communication ⟨KCS v6 Practices Guide⟩ [gen]
- KCS is **demand-driven**: articles are created from actual demand (real questions) rather than anticipated needs, producing just-in-time content that reflects the requestor's context and words ⟨KCS v6 Practices Guide⟩ [gen]

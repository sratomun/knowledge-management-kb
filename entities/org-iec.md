# IEC

## What it is
The International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC) is the international standards body for electrical, electronic and related technologies; jointly with ISO it operates ISO/IEC JTC 1 and co-publishes standards such as ISO/IEC 11179.

## Key facts
- Co-publishes ISO/IEC 11179 (Metadata registries); Part 1: Framework, 4th edition, was published by ISO/IEC effective 2023-01-16 ⟨iso-iec-11179-1⟩

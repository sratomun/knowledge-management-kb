# gistBFO

## What it is
gistBFO is a bridge ontology developed by Semantic Arts that supplements gist to provide alignment with the Basic Formal Ontology (BFO). It lets modelers build with gist while maintaining compliance with BFO.

## Key facts
- gistBFO is a bridge ontology that supplements gist to provide alignment with the Basic Formal Ontology (BFO) ⟨semanticarts.com/gist⟩.
- Using gistBFO, you can get all the benefits of building your ontology with gist while maintaining compliance with BFO ⟨semanticarts.com/gist⟩.
- Of the several gist industry extensions Semantic Arts has developed (cybersecurity, pharmaceuticals, accounting, computing, human resources, professional services), gistBFO is the one that is publicly available on GitHub; most others are available only to clients ⟨semanticarts.com/gist⟩.

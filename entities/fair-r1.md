# FAIR R1

## What it is
The first Reusability sub-principle of the FAIR Guiding Principles: data and metadata should be richly described with a plurality of accurate and relevant attributes so they can be reused in different settings.

## Key facts
- (Meta)data are richly described with a plurality of accurate and relevant attributes ⟨R1⟩

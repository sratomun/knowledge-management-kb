# TriG

## What it is
TriG is a concrete RDF syntax for serializing RDF datasets (and graphs), one of the RDF document syntaxes cited by the abstract syntax.

## Key facts
- TriG is cited as a concrete RDF syntax in which an RDF document encodes an RDF graph or RDF dataset ⟨§1.8⟩.
- RDF documents enable the exchange of RDF graphs and RDF datasets between systems ⟨§1.8⟩.

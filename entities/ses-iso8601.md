# ISO 8601 (date syntax, DCMI SES usage)

## What it is
The ISO date/time interchange standard that DCMI recommends as the syntax for date-valued properties. It is referenced by DCMI rather than coined as a DCMI datatype term.

## Key facts
- dcterms:date recommends expressing the date according to ISO 8601-1, or a published profile such as W3CDTF or EDTF. ⟨§2⟩
- dcterms:extent recommends specifying duration in ISO 8601 format. ⟨§2⟩
- Cited in the bibliography as "[ISO8601-1] ISO 8601-1, Date and time — Representations for information interchange — Part 1: Basic rules." ⟨§9⟩

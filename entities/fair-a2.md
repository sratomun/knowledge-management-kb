# FAIR A2

## What it is
An Accessibility sub-principle of the FAIR Guiding Principles: metadata should remain accessible over the long term, even when the data they describe are no longer available.

## Key facts
- Metadata are accessible, even when the data are no longer available ⟨A2⟩

# PCF Process Group (Level 2)

## What it is
A Process Group is the second level of the PCF hierarchy — a set of related processes
grouped beneath a Category.

## Key facts
- APQC recommends key performance indicators for measuring and tracking the performance of each process group ⟨apqc.org/process-frameworks⟩
- Process Group is Level 2 of the hierarchy, sitting between Category and Process [gen] ⟨apqc.org/process-frameworks⟩

# GO FAIR

## What it is
GO FAIR is the organization that stewards and publishes the FAIR Guiding Principles for scientific data management and stewardship.

## Key facts
- Publishes the FAIR Guiding Principles, guidelines to improve the Findability, Accessibility, Interoperability, and Reuse of digital assets ⟨go-fair.org/fair-principles⟩
- The principles it stewards emphasise machine-actionability so computational systems can find, access, interoperate, and reuse data with none or minimal human intervention ⟨go-fair.org/fair-principles⟩

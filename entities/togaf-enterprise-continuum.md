# TOGAF Enterprise Continuum

## What it is

The Enterprise Continuum is a TOGAF model for classifying, storing, and reusing architecture and solution assets as they evolve from generic foundation architectures to organization-specific ones. [gen]

## Key facts

- The Enterprise Continuum is part of the TOGAF Fundamental Content that supplies the standard's core concepts and practices. ⟨opengroup.org/togaf⟩
- It provides a way to classify and categorize architecture and solution artifacts as they evolve from generic to organization-specific. [gen] ⟨TOGAF⟩
- It comprises two complementary continua: the Architecture Continuum and the Solutions Continuum. [gen] ⟨TOGAF⟩
- Its assets range from generic Foundation Architectures through Common Systems and Industry architectures to Organization-Specific Architectures. [gen] ⟨TOGAF⟩
- It supports reuse of architecture and solution assets across the ADM. [gen] ⟨TOGAF⟩

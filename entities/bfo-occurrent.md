# bfo:Occurrent

## What it is

The occurrent is one of the two top-level categories of Basic Formal Ontology: an entity that unfolds or happens in time, standing opposite the continuant. Occurrents are the processes, process boundaries and temporal/spatiotemporal regions of BFO — the "happenings" rather than the persisting things.

## Key facts

- Occurrent is one of the two mutually exclusive and exhaustive top-level BFO categories, the other being continuant [gen] ⟨basic-formal-ontology.org / Home⟩.
- An occurrent unfolds or happens in time, whereas a continuant persists through time while maintaining its identity [gen] ⟨basic-formal-ontology.org / Home⟩.
- Occurrents include processes, process boundaries, temporal regions and spatiotemporal regions [gen] ⟨basic-formal-ontology.org / Home⟩.
- The occurrent category is a domain-neutral upper-ontology category, containing no terms proper to the special sciences ⟨basic-formal-ontology.org / Home⟩.
- The occurrent category is fixed in the normative BFO 2020 release maintained under the BFO-2020 project ⟨basic-formal-ontology.org / Home → GitHub github.com/bfo-ontology/BFO-2020⟩.

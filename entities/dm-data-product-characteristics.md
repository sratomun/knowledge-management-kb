# Data product characteristics

## What it is
The set of usability traits a domain's analytical dataset must exhibit to be considered a product on the mesh. The canonical six are: discoverable, addressable, trustworthy, self-describing, interoperable, and secure.

## Key facts
- The article enumerates a list of capabilities — including discoverability, security, explorability, understandability, trustworthiness — that a data mesh implementation should support for domain data to be considered a product ⟨martinfowler.com/articles/data-mesh-principles.html, §Data as a product⟩
- The commonly cited six baseline characteristics are discoverable, addressable, trustworthy, self-describing (understandable), interoperable, and secure [gen] ⟨martinfowler.com/articles/data-mesh-principles.html, §Data as a product⟩
- These traits address the friction of discovering, understanding, trusting, and using quality data across a growing number of domains ⟨martinfowler.com/articles/data-mesh-principles.html, §Data as a product⟩
- The data product owner is accountable for the objective measures behind these traits — data quality, decreased lead time of consumption, and user satisfaction ⟨martinfowler.com/articles/data-mesh-principles.html, §Data as a product⟩

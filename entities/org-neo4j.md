# Neo4j

## What it is
Neo4j is a graph-database vendor that has promoted the "GraphRAG" framing — combining knowledge graphs with retrieval-augmented generation — through its GraphRAG Manifesto and related tooling.

## Key facts
- Published the GraphRAG Manifesto arguing that adding a knowledge graph to RAG improves answer accuracy, explainability, and governance versus vector-only RAG [gen] ⟨neo4j-graphrag-manifesto⟩

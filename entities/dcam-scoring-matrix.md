# DCAM — Scoring Matrix

## What it is
The mechanism DCAM uses to measure an organization's data management capabilities and progress, evaluating engagement and processes against auditable evidence to produce a maturity score.

## Key facts
- DCAM measures data management capabilities and progress using a scoring matrix that evaluates engagement and processes with auditable evidence ⟨edmcouncil.org/dcam⟩
- Each of the 34 required capabilities (with 101 sub-capabilities) carries objectives, questions, artifacts of evidence, and criteria for scoring ⟨edmcouncil.org/dcam⟩
- Supports an objective self-assessment or an assessment with a DCAM Authorized Partner to identify gaps and benchmark against peers ⟨edmcouncil.org/dcam⟩
- [gen] The public overview does not enumerate a fixed set of named maturity levels; maturity is expressed as a score of capabilities against evidence ⟨DCAM⟩

# DMBOK Knowledge Area: Metadata Management

## What it is
The knowledge area covering the planning, implementation, and control activities needed to enable access to high-quality, integrated metadata — the data that describes and gives meaning to other data.

## Key facts
- One of the ten knowledge areas surrounding Data Governance on the DAMA Wheel [gen] ⟨DAMA Wheel⟩
- Treated as a distinct knowledge area underpinning data usability and trust across the other functions [gen] ⟨DAMA Wheel⟩
- Described via a consistent context diagram of goals, activities, deliverables, roles, and metrics [gen] ⟨DAMA Wheel⟩

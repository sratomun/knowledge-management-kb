# TOGAF Technology Architecture

## What it is

Technology Architecture is one of the four TOGAF architecture domains; it describes the logical software and hardware capabilities required to support the deployment of business, data, and application services. [gen]

## Key facts

- Technology Architecture is one of the four architecture domains of the TOGAF Standard. [gen] ⟨TOGAF⟩
- It describes the logical software and hardware capabilities required to support the deployment of business, data, and application services — for example IT infrastructure, middleware, networks, processing, and standards. [gen] ⟨TOGAF⟩
- It is developed primarily in Phase D of the ADM. [gen] ⟨TOGAF⟩
- The four architecture domains are commonly treated as a subset of the overall enterprise architecture the TOGAF Standard supports. ⟨opengroup.org/togaf⟩

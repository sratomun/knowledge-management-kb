# index.md / log.md conventions

## What it is

`index.md` and `log.md` are optional reserved filenames within an OKF bundle: index files support navigation of the concept hierarchy, and log files record chronological change history.

## Key facts

- Bundles "can optionally include index.md files (for progressive disclosure as agents navigate the hierarchy)" ⟨How OKF works: The design in one screen⟩
- Bundles can optionally include "log.md files (for chronological history of changes)" ⟨How OKF works: The design in one screen⟩
- These are among "the small number of reserved filenames" covered by the single-page v0.1 specification ⟨How OKF works: The design in one screen⟩
- The pattern echoes "repos full of index.md and log.md artifacts that agents consult before doing real work" ⟨Knowledge as a living wiki⟩

# Organize (APQC KM Framework)

## What it is
The step of the APQC knowledge flow process (also framed as Organize/Review) in which collected knowledge is structured, curated, validated, and kept current so it remains findable and trustworthy.

## Key facts
- Organize/Review keeps knowledge findable and trustworthy within the seven-step knowledge flow [gen] ⟨APQC KM Framework⟩
- Review and curation help ensure knowledge remains current, supporting reliable reuse ⟨apqc.org/expertise/knowledge-management⟩

# Collect (APQC KM Framework)

## What it is
The step of the APQC knowledge flow process in which knowledge is captured from people, teams, and systems into a reusable form.

## Key facts
- Collect is the capture step feeding Organize/Review in the seven-step knowledge flow [gen] ⟨APQC KM Framework⟩
- Knowledge sharing includes more than publishing documents; it moves useful know-how from people, teams, and systems to those who need it, which begins with capture ⟨apqc.org/expertise/knowledge-management⟩

# Create (APQC KM Framework)

## What it is
The first step of the APQC knowledge flow process, in which new knowledge is generated through work, experience, research, problem-solving, and collaboration.

## Key facts
- Create is the first step of the seven-step knowledge flow; the loop re-enters here after Use/Apply generates new knowledge [gen] ⟨APQC KM Framework⟩
- Knowledge Management exists to help information and knowledge flow to and between the right people at the right time, of which creation is the origin ⟨apqc.org/expertise/knowledge-management⟩

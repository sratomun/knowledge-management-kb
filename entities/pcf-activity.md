# PCF Activity (Level 4)

## What it is
An Activity is the fourth level of the PCF hierarchy — a key event or step performed when
executing a Process.

## Key facts
- Activity is Level 4 of the hierarchy, decomposing a Process into its key events/steps and sitting above Task [gen] ⟨apqc.org/process-frameworks⟩

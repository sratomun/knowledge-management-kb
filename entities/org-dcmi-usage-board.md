# DCMI Usage Board

## What it is
The DCMI Usage Board is the body within DCMI responsible for approving term definitions and issuing the DCMI Metadata Terms specification.

## Key facts
- Issued the DCMI Metadata Terms as a DCMI Recommendation on 2020-01-20, licensed CC BY 4.0 ⟨dcmi-terms⟩

# ISO 30401:2018/Amd 1:2022

## What it is
A 2022 amendment to ISO 30401:2018 that updates the standard, primarily to align its structure and text with the current ISO Harmonized Structure used across ISO management-system standards.

## Key facts
- Updates the standard's structure and text toward the current ISO Harmonized Structure. ⟨iso.org/standard/68683⟩ [gen]
- Is a maintenance amendment to the 2018 first edition rather than a new edition. ⟨iso.org/standard/68683⟩ [gen]

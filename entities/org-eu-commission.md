# European Commission

## What it is
The European Commission is the EU's executive body; in this corpus it is the originator/steward of binding EU regulations (the AI Act, GDPR) and of the ESCO classification.

## Key facts
- Steward of the EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) and GDPR (Regulation (EU) 2016/679) as binding EU law [gen] ⟨eu-ai-act⟩
- Maintains ESCO, the multilingual classification of skills, competences, qualifications and occupations, as linked open data [gen] ⟨esco⟩

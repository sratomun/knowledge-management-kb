# RDF 1.1 Turtle

## What it is
Turtle (Terse RDF Triple Language) is a concrete serialization syntax for storing and exchanging RDF, one of the RDF-based specifications linked by the abstract syntax.

## Key facts
- Turtle is cited as a serialization syntax for storing and exchanging RDF ⟨§1⟩.
- An RDF document is a document that encodes an RDF graph or RDF dataset in a concrete RDF syntax, such as Turtle ⟨§1.8⟩.
- A concrete RDF syntax such as Turtle may offer many different ways to encode the same RDF graph or dataset, but these aspects are not significant for its meaning ⟨§1.8⟩.

# Zhamak Dehghani

## What it is
Zhamak Dehghani is the practitioner who originated the data mesh paradigm and authored the O'Reilly book Data Mesh.

## Key facts
- Authored Data Mesh (O'Reilly, 2022) and the canonical article "Data Mesh Principles and Logical Architecture" (martinfowler.com, 2020) that defines the four data-mesh principles ⟨data-mesh-dehghani⟩

# dcterms:rights

## What it is
A core Dublin Core element carrying information about rights held in and over the resource.

## Key facts
- URI `http://purl.org/dc/terms/rights`; definition "Information about rights held in and over the resource." ⟨§2⟩
- Typically includes a statement about intellectual property rights; recommended practice is to refer to a rights statement with a URI. ⟨§2⟩
- Range Includes `RightsStatement`; subproperty of `dc:rights`. ⟨§2⟩

# ISO/DIS 30401 (revision)

## What it is
The Draft International Standard for the revision of ISO 30401, representing work in progress toward a second edition of the knowledge management systems standard.

## Key facts
- Is the DIS (Draft International Standard) stage of the ISO 30401 revision toward a second edition. ⟨iso.org/standard/68683⟩ [gen]
- Is a draft under development and not yet a published edition. ⟨iso.org/standard/68683⟩ [gen]

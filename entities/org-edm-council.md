# EDM Council

## What it is
The EDM Council (Enterprise Data Management Council, now EDM Association) is the industry body that develops and publishes DCAM, a framework for assessing and benchmarking data management and analytics capabilities.

## Key facts
- Publishes DCAM (Data Management Capability Assessment Model), described as the world's most adopted data management and analytics framework ⟨edmcouncil.org/dcam⟩
- DCAM is developed and maintained via collaboration of EDM Council (EDM Association) members and is available exclusively to member firms ⟨edmcouncil.org/dcam⟩

# ISO/IEC 11179-5 — Naming and identification principles

## What it is
Part 5 sets principles for naming and uniquely identifying administered items within the register.

## Key facts
- Each data element is uniquely identified within the register per Part 5 ⟨Wikipedia: ISO/IEC 11179⟩
- A data element should be named according to the Naming and Identification Principles (Part 5) ⟨Wikipedia: ISO/IEC 11179⟩
- Published as ISO/IEC 11179-5:2015 ⟨Wikipedia: ISO/IEC 11179⟩

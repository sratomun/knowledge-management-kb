# DCMIType Text

## What it is
A DCMI Type Vocabulary class for a resource consisting primarily of words for reading.

## Key facts
- URI `http://purl.org/dc/dcmitype/Text`; definition "A resource consisting primarily of words for reading." ⟨§7⟩
- Comment: examples include books, letters, dissertations, poems, newspapers, articles; facsimiles or images of texts are still of the genre Text. ⟨§7⟩
- Member Of `DCMIType`. ⟨§7⟩

# ISO 30401 management-system clauses (4-10)

## What it is
The auditable requirement clauses of ISO 30401:2018, organized per the ISO Harmonized Structure: Context of the organization (4), Leadership (5), Planning (6), Support (7), Operation (8), Performance evaluation (9), and Improvement (10). Clauses 1-3 (scope, normative references, terms) are front matter and not auditable requirements.

## Key facts
- Follow the ISO Harmonized (High-Level) Structure common to all ISO management-system standards. ⟨iso.org/standard/68683⟩ [gen]
- Can be mapped to a KMS design framework to help an organization align its KM program with the standard. ⟨RealKM (Shekar, 2022)⟩
- Broadly follow the typical KM execution lifecycle: initiation/sponsorship, planning, design of practices/processes/IT, implementation, go-live support, and measurement of usage and effectiveness. ⟨RealKM (Shekar, 2022)⟩
- Clause 10 (Improvement) drives continuous improvement of the KMS as user feedback is channeled back into systematic development. ⟨RealKM (Shekar, 2022)⟩

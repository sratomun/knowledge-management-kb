# PCF Process (Level 3)

## What it is
A Process is the third level of the PCF hierarchy — an individual end-to-end business
process nested within a Process Group.

## Key facts
- The PCF contains hundreds of processes, each tied to an APQC-authored process definition ⟨apqc.org/process-frameworks⟩
- APQC's process definitions align each process to global terminology to help organizations identify and align their activities ⟨apqc.org/process-frameworks⟩
- Process is Level 3 of the hierarchy, sitting between Process Group and Activity [gen] ⟨apqc.org/process-frameworks⟩

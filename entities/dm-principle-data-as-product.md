# Data as a product

## What it is
The second data mesh principle: apply product thinking to a domain's analytical data, treating that data as a product and its consumers as customers.

## Key facts
- Analytical data provided by domains must be treated as a product, and the consumers of that data treated as customers — happy and delighted customers ⟨martinfowler.com/articles/data-mesh-principles.html, §Data as a product⟩
- Addresses the high friction and cost of discovering, understanding, trusting, and using quality data — the "dark data" problem (Gartner) ⟨martinfowler.com/articles/data-mesh-principles.html, §Data as a product⟩
- Introduces the domain data product owner role, accountable for objective measures: data quality, decreased lead time of consumption, and user satisfaction (net promoter score) ⟨martinfowler.com/articles/data-mesh-principles.html, §Data as a product⟩
- Inverts responsibility versus past paradigms: accountability for data quality shifts upstream, as close to the source of the data as possible ⟨martinfowler.com/articles/data-mesh-principles.html, §Data as a product⟩
- So that data users can easily discover, understand and securely use high quality data distributed across many domains with a delightful experience ⟨martinfowler.com/articles/data-mesh-principles.html, §Principles Summary and the high level logical architecture⟩

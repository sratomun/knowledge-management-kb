# ISO/IEC 11179-4 — Formulation of data definitions

## What it is
Part 4 sets out rules and principles for forming clear, precise data definitions for registry content.

## Key facts
- Part 4 describes principles for forming data definitions ⟨Wikipedia: ISO/IEC 11179⟩
- A data element should be defined by the Formulation of Data Definitions rules (Part 4) ⟨Wikipedia: ISO/IEC 11179⟩
- An Edition 3 of Part 4 has not been proposed ⟨Wikipedia: ISO/IEC 11179⟩

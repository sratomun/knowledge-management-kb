# FAIR R1.1

## What it is
A Reusability sub-principle of the FAIR Guiding Principles refining R1: data and metadata should be released with a clear and accessible data usage license.

## Key facts
- (Meta)data are released with a clear and accessible data usage license ⟨R1.1⟩

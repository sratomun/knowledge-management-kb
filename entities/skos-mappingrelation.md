# skos:mappingRelation

## What it is
The super-property of the SKOS mapping (alignment) properties, which state links between SKOS concepts in different concept schemes where the link is inherent in the meaning of the linked concepts.

## Key facts
- skos:mappingRelation is an instance of owl:ObjectProperty ⟨S38⟩.
- skos:mappingRelation is a sub-property of skos:semanticRelation ⟨S39⟩.
- skos:closeMatch, skos:broadMatch, skos:narrowMatch and skos:relatedMatch are each sub-properties of skos:mappingRelation ⟨S40⟩.

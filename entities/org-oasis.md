# OASIS

## What it is
OASIS (Organization for the Advancement of Structured Information Standards) is a standards body that develops open, structured-information standards; in this corpus it stewards the LegalDocML (Akoma Ntoso) and LegalRuleML standards.

## Key facts
- Publishes Akoma Ntoso (LegalDocML) as an OASIS Standard for the XML representation of legislative, judicial, and parliamentary documents ⟨akoma-ntoso⟩
- Publishes LegalRuleML as an OASIS Standard for the machine-readable representation of legal rules and norms ⟨legalruleml⟩

# DMBOK Knowledge Area: Reference & Master Data

## What it is
The knowledge area covering the ongoing reconciliation and maintenance of core shared data — combining Reference Data Management (codes and lookup values) and Master Data Management (authoritative versions of key business entities).

## Key facts
- One of the ten knowledge areas surrounding Data Governance on the DAMA Wheel [gen] ⟨DAMA Wheel⟩
- Unifies Reference Data Management and Master Data Management as a single Wheel knowledge area [gen] ⟨DAMA Wheel⟩
- Described via a consistent context diagram of goals, activities, deliverables, roles, and metrics [gen] ⟨DAMA Wheel⟩

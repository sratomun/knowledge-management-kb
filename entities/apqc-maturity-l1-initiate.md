# APQC KM Maturity — Level 1: Initiate

## What it is
The first level of APQC's Levels of Knowledge Management Maturity, describing a KM program at its earliest, most immature state where activity is ad hoc and inconsistent. [gen] The public page did not enumerate the levels; this description is general knowledge of APQC's model, not the gated resource text.

## Key facts
- [gen] KM activity is nascent, ad hoc, and inconsistent; the organization begins recognizing the value of KM but lacks defined processes or dedicated ownership ⟨apqc.org/resource-library/resource-listing/apqcs-levels-of-knowledge-management-maturity⟩
- The model as a whole is a road map for moving from immature, inconsistent KM activities toward mature, disciplined approaches; Initiate is its starting point ⟨apqc.org/resource-library/resource-listing/apqcs-levels-of-knowledge-management-maturity⟩

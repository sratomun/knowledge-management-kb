# rdfs:comment

## What it is
`rdfs:comment` is the property used to provide a human-readable description of a resource.

## Key facts
- "rdfs:comment is an instance of rdf:Property that may be used to provide a human-readable description of a resource." ⟨§3.7⟩
- The `rdfs:domain` of `rdfs:comment` is `rdfs:Resource` and its `rdfs:range` is `rdfs:Literal` ⟨§3.7⟩.
- "Multilingual documentation is supported through use of the language tagging facility of RDF literals." ⟨§3.7⟩

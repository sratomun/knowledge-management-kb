# TOGAF Business Architecture

## What it is

Business Architecture is one of the four TOGAF architecture domains; it describes the enterprise's business strategy, governance, organization, and key business processes. [gen]

## Key facts

- TOGAF Version 9.2 included enhancements to the Business Architecture that made the framework easier to use and maintain. ⟨opengroup.org/togaf⟩
- Business Architecture is one of the four architecture domains addressed by the TOGAF Standard. [gen] ⟨TOGAF⟩
- It describes the business strategy, governance, organization, and key business processes of the enterprise. [gen] ⟨TOGAF⟩
- It is developed primarily in Phase B of the ADM. [gen] ⟨TOGAF⟩

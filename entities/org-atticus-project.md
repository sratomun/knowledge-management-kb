# The Atticus Project

## What it is
The Atticus Project is the legal non-profit whose volunteer experts created CUAD, an expert-annotated dataset for legal contract review.

## Key facts
- Created CUAD (Contract Understanding Atticus Dataset) with dozens of legal experts, comprising over 13,000 annotations ⟨cuad⟩

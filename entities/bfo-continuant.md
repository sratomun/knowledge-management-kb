# bfo:Continuant

## What it is

The continuant is one of the two top-level categories of Basic Formal Ontology: an entity that persists through time while maintaining its identity, standing opposite the occurrent. Continuants are the "things" of BFO (objects, their parts and aggregates, and the qualities, roles and dispositions that depend on them), as distinct from the processes that happen to or involve them.

## Key facts

- BFO is "a small, upper level ontology" whose categories, including continuant, are designed to support information retrieval, analysis and integration across scientific and other domains ⟨basic-formal-ontology.org / Home⟩.
- Continuant is one of the two mutually exclusive and exhaustive top-level BFO categories, the other being occurrent [gen] ⟨basic-formal-ontology.org / Home⟩.
- A continuant persists through time while maintaining its identity, whereas an occurrent unfolds or happens in time [gen] ⟨basic-formal-ontology.org / Home⟩.
- Continuants subdivide into independent continuants, specifically dependent continuants and generically dependent continuants [gen] ⟨basic-formal-ontology.org / Home⟩.
- The continuant category is fixed in the normative BFO 2020 release, whose sources are maintained under the BFO-2020 project ⟨basic-formal-ontology.org / Home → GitHub github.com/bfo-ontology/BFO-2020⟩.

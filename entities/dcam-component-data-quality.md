# DCAM — Data Quality component

## What it is
A DCAM framework capability component covering the practices and controls that keep data fit for purpose. Data quality is one of the areas DCAM assessments evaluate and strengthen.

## Key facts
- Case-study use frames DCAM as strengthening governance, data quality, architecture, and operating models in support of business growth and regulatory confidence ⟨edmcouncil.org/dcam⟩
- DCAM enables organizations to achieve greater efficiency and accuracy with better managed data to meet compliance and reporting requirements ⟨edmcouncil.org/dcam⟩
- [gen] Earlier public DCAM materials framed Data Quality as one of the model's named components ⟨DCAM⟩

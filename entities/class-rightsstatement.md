# dcterms:RightsStatement

## What it is
A DCMI class for a statement about the rights held in or over a resource; the value type for rights and accessRights.

## Key facts
- URI `http://purl.org/dc/terms/RightsStatement`; Label "Rights Statement". ⟨§6⟩
- Definition "A statement about the intellectual property rights (IPR) held in or over a resource, a legal document giving official permission to do something with a resource, or a statement about access rights." ⟨§6⟩
- Superclass of `LicenseDocument`; named in Range Includes of dcterms:rights and dcterms:accessRights. ⟨§6⟩

# W3C RDF Working Group

## What it is
The W3C RDF Working Group is the W3C working group that produced the RDF 1.1 specification suite, including RDF 1.1 Concepts and Abstract Syntax.

## Key facts
- Produced RDF 1.1 Concepts and Abstract Syntax, published as a W3C Recommendation on 25 February 2014 ⟨rdf-11-concepts⟩

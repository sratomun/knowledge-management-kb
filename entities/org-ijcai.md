# IJCAI

## What it is
IJCAI (the International Joint Conferences on Artificial Intelligence) is the organization behind the IJCAI conference series, which published the "Ontology-Based Data Access: A Survey" paper.

## Key facts
- Published "Ontology-Based Data Access: A Survey" (Xiao et al.) in the survey track of IJCAI 2018, pp. 5511-5519 ⟨xiao-obda-survey⟩

# ISO 30401 Annex B (KM and adjacent disciplines)

## What it is
An informative annex of ISO 30401:2018 describing the relationship between knowledge management and adjacent or related disciplines, positioning KM relative to neighbouring practices.

## Key facts
- Addresses the relationship between knowledge management and adjacent disciplines. ⟨iso.org/standard/68683⟩ [gen]
- Is informative (guidance), not part of the auditable requirement clauses. ⟨iso.org/standard/68683⟩ [gen]

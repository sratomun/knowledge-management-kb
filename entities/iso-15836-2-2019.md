# ISO 15836-2:2019

## What it is
The ISO standardization of the most useful properties and classes of DCMI Metadata Terms beyond the original fifteen elements.

## Key facts
- "The most useful properties and classes of DCMI Metadata Terms have now been published as ISO 15836-2:2019." ⟨§1⟩
- Reference URL `https://www.iso.org/standard/71341.html`. ⟨§1 References⟩
- Associated with the DCMI /terms/ namespace, whose use DCMI gently encourages. ⟨§1⟩

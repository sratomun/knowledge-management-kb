# ISO/IEC 11179-1 — Framework

## What it is
Part 1 (Framework) introduces the ISO/IEC 11179 metadata registry standard and provides the conceptual foundation, explaining the purpose and role of each of the other parts.

## Key facts
- Part 1 explains the purpose of each part of the standard ⟨Wikipedia: ISO/IEC 11179⟩
- The standard has two main purposes: definition and exchange ⟨Wikipedia: ISO/IEC 11179⟩
- The 2023 publication is the current (Edition 4) of Part 1: Framework, effective from 2023-01-16 ⟨iso.org/standard/78914⟩

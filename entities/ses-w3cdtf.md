# W3CDTF (W3C Date and Time Formats SES)

## What it is
A DCMI syntax encoding scheme (datatype) for dates and times formatted per the W3C Date and Time Formats specification.

## Key facts
- URI `http://purl.org/dc/terms/W3CDTF`; Label "W3C-DTF". ⟨§5⟩
- Definition "The set of dates and times constructed according to the W3C Date and Time Formats Specification." ⟨§5⟩
- See `http://www.w3.org/TR/NOTE-datetime`. ⟨§5⟩

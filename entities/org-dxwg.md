# W3C Dataset Exchange Working Group

## What it is
The W3C Dataset Exchange Working Group (DXWG) is the working group that produced DCAT 3, the Data Catalog Vocabulary.

## Key facts
- Produced DCAT 3, the W3C Recommendation of 22 August 2024 — a major revision of DCAT 2 that adds versioning, dataset series, and checksum support while preserving backward compatibility ⟨dcat-3⟩

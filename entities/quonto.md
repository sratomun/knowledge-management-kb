# QuOnto

## What it is
QuOnto is a reasoning system for ontologies of the DL-Lite family on top of which the
authors implement their ontology-based data access solution, enhancing it to reason over
DL-LiteA ontologies together with mappings to external relational databases.

## Key facts
- The paper's solution is being implemented on top of the QuOnto system, a tool for reasoning over ontologies of the DL-Lite family ⟨§7⟩
- QuOnto was originally based on DL-LiteF, a DL that does not distinguish between data and objects ⟨§7⟩
- By enhancing QuOnto with the ability to reason over both DL-LiteA ontologies and mappings, the authors obtain a complete system for ontology-based data access ⟨§7⟩

# Benchmarking

## What it is
Benchmarking is the practice of objectively measuring an organization's performance and comparing it against others — internally across units, or externally against other organizations — to locate gaps and opportunities. It depends on a shared frame of reference: when everyone describes their processes and measures in the same terms, comparisons across teams and industries become meaningful.

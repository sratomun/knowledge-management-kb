# Data Governance

## What it is

Data governance is the exercise of authority, control, and shared decision-making over the management of data assets — the policies, standards, roles, and oversight that determine how data is defined, who is accountable for it, and how rules about it are set and enforced. It is the coordinating function that gives the rest of data management its direction.

## Where sources differ

Only DAMA-DMBOK2 is cited here for governance as a general concept, so no cross-source divergence is recorded. Note, however, that a contrasting governance model — federated computational governance — is described elsewhere in the corpus by Data Mesh (Dehghani), which inverts the centralized, hub-coordinated arrangement DMBOK2 depicts; that divergence is captured on the [[federated-computational-governance]] page rather than resolved here.

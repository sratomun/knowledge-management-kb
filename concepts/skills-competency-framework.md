# Skills / competency framework

## What it is
A structured vocabulary of skills, competences, knowledge, and abilities, often linked to occupations, used to describe what work requires and to support matching, career pathways, and workforce analytics.

## How sources treat it
- **[[onet]]** _(standard · normative)_ — describes each occupation through the O*NET Content Model with structured descriptors for knowledge, skills, abilities (KSAs), tasks, tools/technology, and work activities ⟨O*NET Content Model⟩ [gen]
- **[[esco]]** _(standard · normative)_ — publishes skills/competences as an interlinked pillar with multilingual preferred/alternative labels for cross-border matching ⟨ESCO documentation, three pillars⟩ [gen]

## Where sources differ
O*NET expresses requirements as descriptor scales tied to occupations; ESCO expresses skills as linked-data concepts with multilingual labels. The two organize similar content differently rather than disagreeing on it.

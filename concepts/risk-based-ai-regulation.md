# Risk-based AI regulation

## What it is
A regulatory design that sorts uses of AI into tiers of risk and attaches obligations proportionate to each tier, rather than regulating all AI uniformly. Prohibited uses sit at the top, high-risk uses carry the heaviest compliance requirements, and low-risk uses are largely unregulated.

## How sources treat it
- **[[eu-ai-act]]** _(regulation · binding)_ — sorts AI uses into unacceptable-risk (prohibited), high-risk (permitted subject to requirements), limited-risk (transparency), and minimal-risk tiers, with obligations proportionate to risk ⟨Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, risk framework⟩ [gen]
- **[[eu-ai-act]]** _(regulation · binding)_ — high-risk systems carry obligations such as risk management, data governance, technical documentation, logging, human oversight, and accuracy/robustness/cybersecurity ⟨Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Title III⟩ [gen]

## Where sources differ
Only one binding source treats this directly here; it is complementary to voluntary risk-management guidance such as [[ai-risk-management]] rather than in conflict with it.

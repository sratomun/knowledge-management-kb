# Data Management Maturity

## What it is

Data management maturity is the degree to which an organization's data management capabilities are established, evidenced, and consistently practiced, expressed on a scale so that the organization can locate where it stands, identify gaps, and define a roadmap toward a target state. It is typically measured by assessing capabilities against a capability/assessment model and scoring the evidence, which also enables benchmarking against peers or industry best practice.

## Where sources differ

Only the EDM DCAM source is cited here, and it treats maturity as a score of capabilities against auditable evidence rather than as a fixed set of named maturity levels; the public overview it draws on does not enumerate a level scale. As with the capability model, the account is drawn from the public overview page plus general knowledge (marked [gen]), because the full framework is members-only and was not consulted.

# Process Classification

## What it is
Process classification is the practice of arranging an organization's business processes into a hierarchical taxonomy — categories, groups, processes, activities, and tasks — so that they can be named consistently, related to one another, and compared across teams and industries. A shared classification gives organizations a common language and a ready-made basis for benchmarking, content management, and process management instead of starting from scratch.

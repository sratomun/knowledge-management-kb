# HR data interoperability

## What it is
The ability of human-resources systems to exchange data reliably, achieved through shared, vendor-neutral schemas and classifications for HR objects and processes, reducing point-to-point integration cost.

## How sources treat it
- **[[hr-open-standards]]** _(standard · normative)_ — publishes free, vendor-neutral data-exchange schemas for HR business processes (recruiting, assessment, staffing, payroll, benefits), the successor to HR-XML ⟨HR Open Standards documentation⟩ [gen]
- **[[esco]]** _(standard · normative)_ — is published as linked open data with a SPARQL endpoint and stable URIs, enabling interoperable reference to occupations and skills ⟨ESCO linked data⟩ [gen]

## Where sources differ
HR Open Standards targets message/schema interchange between systems; ESCO targets a shared reference classification. They operate at different layers and are complementary.

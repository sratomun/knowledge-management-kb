# Occupation taxonomy

## What it is
A structured classification of occupations, usually cross-walked to a national or international standard classification, used to organize labour-market and workforce information and to enable matching across systems.

## How sources treat it
- **[[onet]]** _(standard · normative)_ — organizes work using the O*NET-SOC taxonomy, derived from and cross-walked to the federal Standard Occupational Classification (SOC) ⟨O*NET documentation, occupational taxonomy⟩ [gen]
- **[[esco]]** _(standard · normative)_ — structures occupations as one of three pillars and maps them to the international ISCO-08 classification for interoperability ⟨ESCO documentation, ISCO mapping⟩ [gen]

## Where sources differ
O*NET is U.S.-oriented and anchored to SOC; ESCO is EU-oriented, multilingual, and anchored to ISCO-08. Both are complementary regional classifications rather than competing definitions.

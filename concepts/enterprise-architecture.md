# Enterprise Architecture

## What it is

Enterprise architecture (EA) is the discipline of describing, planning, and governing the structure of an enterprise — its business, information, applications, and technology — as a coherent whole, so that change can be steered toward strategic goals rather than accreting ad hoc. It is supported by frameworks and methods that supply common concepts, a repeatable process, and shared vocabulary for the architects who practice it.

## Where sources differ

# KM Enablers

## What it is
KM enablers are the conditions surrounding a knowledge flow process that make it sustainable — the strategy, people and culture, process, content and technology, and governance and measurement that keep knowledge moving beyond any single tool. They express the idea that a KM program requires attention well beyond technology if knowledge is actually to flow and be reused.

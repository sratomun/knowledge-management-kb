# Knowledge Flow Process

## What it is
The knowledge flow process is the sequence of steps by which knowledge moves to and between the right people at the right time — from generating new knowledge, through capturing and organizing it, to sharing, finding, and applying it in work. Applying knowledge in turn produces new knowledge, so the process is a loop rather than a one-way pipeline.

# Data Management

## What it is

Data management is the overall discipline of developing, executing, and supervising the plans, policies, programs, and practices that deliver, control, protect, and increase the value of data and information assets across their lifecycle. It is the umbrella under which more specialized functions — governance, quality, metadata, storage, security, and the rest — sit.

- **[[dama-dmbok2]]** _(whitepaper · informational)_ — It helps organizations structure, govern, and optimize data assets, aligning them with business strategy, regulatory compliance, and emerging technologies such as AI, machine learning, and cloud computing ⟨dama.org⟩
- **[[dama-dmbok2]]** _(whitepaper · informational)_ — It is vendor-neutral and does not prescribe specific tools, technologies, or methodologies; organizations must adapt it to their unique environments ⟨dama.org⟩

## Where sources differ

Only DAMA-DMBOK2 is cited here, so no cross-source divergence is recorded. DMBOK2 itself frames data management as a consensus-based, deliberately non-prescriptive body of knowledge — a scope-defining reference rather than a standard, certification, or one-size-fits-all method to be applied verbatim.

# Master and Reference Data

## What it is

Master data is the shared, foundational data about core business entities — customers, products, employees, accounts — that many systems and processes depend on. Reference data is the sets of permitted values (code lists, classifications, look-up tables) used to categorize other data. Managing them well means maintaining consistent, authoritative versions that the whole organization can trust.

## Where sources differ

# KM Maturity Model

## What it is
A KM maturity model is a staged framework that describes how a knowledge management program develops from immature, ad hoc activity toward mature, disciplined, strategically aligned practice. It gives organizations a road map — with named levels, milestones, and checkpoints — for locating where their KM program currently stands and planning the next advancement.

- **[[apqc-km-maturity]]** _(whitepaper · informational)_ — the levels are Initiate, Develop, Standardize, Optimize, and Innovate, with organizations progressing through them sequentially ⟨apqc.org/resource-library/resource-listing/apqcs-levels-of-knowledge-management-maturity⟩
